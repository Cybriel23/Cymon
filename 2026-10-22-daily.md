## 1. Risk Watch

### 1.1 eMudhra intermediate CA private keys compromised (NEW, DEVELOPING)
Eight eMudhra intermediate CA private keys were reported to root programs on **17 Oct** and confirmed publicly by Mozilla on **20 Oct**. Reports say the keys were trivially weak (integers below 256 instead of random 384-bit values), traced to a faulty script used in an April 2025 key ceremony; anyone could derive them and sign certificates the chain would accept. Chrome and Firefox blocked the eight certificates on 17 Oct; Apple (19 Oct) and Microsoft (20 Oct) followed; Mozilla is removing the emSign ECC Root CA - G3 root. Chrome says no TLS certificate chaining to the six affected TLS intermediates was logged to Certificate Transparency before the block, and whether the keys were abused is unknown. Go shipped a fix (reported as **CVE-2026-111943**) rejecting chains with the intermediates. eMudhra estimates about **30,000** customer certificates need replacing. Details come mostly from secondary outlets and eMudhra's own statement.
**Action:** Update browsers, OS trust stores and runtimes (Go and others) and inventory non-browser clients that ignore revocation data (Java, embedded devices, agents, internal PKI tooling); if you hold eMudhra/emSign-issued certificates, reissue from another CA; consider explicitly distrusting the eight intermediates; look for certificates in your environment chaining to them.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/eight-emudhra-ca-keys-compromised.html) · [Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/10/21/emudhra-certificates-private-keys-compromised/) · [Scott Helme](https://scotthelme.co.uk/eight-bits-of-trust-emudhras-ca-keys-cracked-in-milliseconds/) · [eMudhra statement](https://emudhra.com/en/post/certificate-security-incident) · [Rescana](https://www.rescana.com/post/emudhra-intermediate-ca-key-compromise-october-2026)

### 1.2 PEERSTITCH implant on FortiGate via CVE-2025-61624 (NEW, KEV)
Unit 42 describes **PEERSTITCH**, a memory-resident ARM64 implant injected into FortiOS daemons (**/bin/miglogd**, **/bin/iked**). It leaves no binary on disk, opens no listening port and makes no outbound connection, instead piggybacking on traffic of legitimate processes. The intrusion at a telecom managed service provider started with **CVE-2024-47575** (FortiManager), exposing configurations and credentials for **500+** managed FortiGates, then used the FortiOS auto-script path traversal **CVE-2025-61624** (CVSS about 6.0 to 6.5, Medium) to deploy the implant. Unit 42 tracks the cluster as **CL-STA-0954** (UNC5820), with post-exploitation overlap with suspected nation-state intrusions. CISA added the CVE to KEV on **16 Oct** (deadline 19 Oct, per secondary reporting; confirm in the catalog). Fortinet fixed it in April 2026 (FG-IR-26-122): **FortiOS 7.6.5 / 7.4.10**. One outlet cites 15K firewalls, which conflicts with the 500+ figure; treat as unverified.
**Action:** Upgrade, and because the implant is memory-only, rely on Unit 42's detection guidance and a reboot plus config/credential rotation for any firewall managed by a historically vulnerable FortiManager; rotate credentials and keys stored in FortiManager.
Sources: [Unit 42](https://unit42.paloaltonetworks.com/fortimanager-cve-2024-47575-peerstitch-malware.md) · [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/attackers-exploit-fortios-flaw-to.html) · [Cloud Security Alliance](https://labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/csa-research-note-peerstitch-fortios-memory-resident-implant/) · [Rescana](https://www.rescana.com/post/fortinet-fortios-cve-2025-61624-peerstitch-kev)

### 1.3 ConnectWise Cloud Backup (SkyKick) credential abuse in Microsoft 365 tenants (DEVELOPING, follow-up)
Huntress saw suspicious sign-ins from **15 Oct** using Microsoft Graph tokens tied to the Cloud Backup application identity; counts rose from 162 to about **173** tenants. ConnectWise disabled seven application identities and revoked certificates on **17 Oct**, suspending Cloud Backup (no backups, no restores; SaaS Security and Cloud Management also paused as a precaution). Microsoft Threat Intelligence calls it an active software supply-chain attack. ConnectWise says archives are encrypted and intact, has not said how the credential was stolen, attributed it, or given a restoration date. No new count found today.
**Action:** Review Entra ID sign-in and audit logs from 15 Oct for the ConnectWise/SkyKick enterprise apps, revoke refresh tokens, hunt for Graph access to mail/SharePoint/OneDrive from hosting-provider IPs, and arrange alternative backups.
Sources: [Huntress](https://www.huntress.com/blog/vendor-credential-enables-unauthorized-access) · [Born City](https://borncity.com/news/connectwise-cloud-backup-dienste-nach-identitaetsverletzungen-ausgesetzt/) · [Techzine](https://www.techzine.eu/news/security/145071/connectwise-shuts-down-cloud-backup-after-hack-on-microsoft-365-tenants/) · [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/connectwise-suspends-cloud-backup-after.html)
