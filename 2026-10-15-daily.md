## Executive Summary
Tempo is high and dominated by edge-device exploitation. Four items matter most today. (1) **Ivanti Connect Secure zero-day CVE-2026-104935** was disclosed on 13 Oct after exploitation by **UTA0576** (Volexity's tracking name) since about 1 to 3 Oct; it is chained with a second patched flaw and a **still-unpatched** privilege escalation, leaves no webshell, and the Integrity Checker may not detect it. KEV due date is reportedly **16 Oct**. (2) **Microsoft's record October Patch Tuesday** (about **1,411 to 1,413** CVEs) includes one exploited zero-day, **CVE-2026-96951** (Windows Shell Mark-of-the-Web bypass), also with a reported **16 Oct** deadline. (4) **SonicWall SMA1000 CVE-2026-102255** (CVSS **10.0**) is still under exploitation attempts with no vendor confirmation. CISA's 14 Oct KEV additions (PAN-OS, Cisco Secure Email Gateway, Ahsay) add further edge-device pressure, though two of the four CVE IDs could not be corroborated. Also open: the IDCF Cloud ransomware outage in Japan, now in its ninth day. Caveat: most sources were reached through search summaries because several primary sites were unreachable; confirm details against vendor advisories. Watch this week for: Ivanti's patch for the third flaw, a SonicWall advisory or KEV update, CISA's formal KEV entries for Ahsay, IDCF recovery news, and more Patch Tuesday exploitation reports.

## 1. Risk Watch

### 1.1 Ivanti Connect Secure zero-day chain, CVE-2026-104935 (NEW, EXPLOITED)
Ivanti disclosed on **13 Oct** a zero-day exploited before disclosure; CISA added it to KEV the same day. CVSS **8.6** (Ivanti). Volexity attributes the activity to **UTA0576**, which chained this flaw with two others to get unauthenticated root code execution on fully patched appliances. Exploitation dates back to about **3 Oct** (Volexity), with Defused seeing signs from **1 Oct**. Two flaws in the chain are patched, but a **privilege escalation bug remains unpatched** and Ivanti is still working on it. The actor reportedly drops no malware or webshell, re-exploits the device for each command, and tampers with logs; CISA warns the Integrity Checker Tool may miss it because commands run in memory. Affected versions differ by source (BleepingComputer: 22.8R2.4 and earlier, 25.1.2.2 and earlier; Security Affairs: before 25.1.3.0; Canadian Cyber Centre lists 25.2.2 and prior), so check Ivanti's advisory. Shadowserver counts over **5,500** exposed ICS instances. Rapid7 says part of the issue was fixed in the July **25.1.3.0** release.
**Action:** Upgrade to the fixed release per Ivanti's advisory now; restrict internet exposure of the management and VPN interfaces where possible; do not rely on the Integrity Checker alone. Preserve network and memory forensics (CISA's recommendation) and hunt for anomalous admin sessions, rotate credentials and certificates stored on the appliance, and watch for Ivanti's update on the unpatched escalation flaw.
Sources: [Rapid7](https://www.rapid7.com/blog/post/etr-ivanti-connect-secure-zero-day-exploited-in-the-wild-cve-2026-104935/) · [BleepingComputer](https://bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/ivanti-warns-of-connect-secure-zero-day-exploited-in-attacks) · [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/attacker-chains-three-ivanti-connect.html) · [CyberScoop](https://cyberscoop.com/ivanti-connect-secure-zero-day-actively-exploited/) · [Canadian Cyber Centre AV26-1035](https://cyber.gc.ca/en/alerts-advisories/ivanti-security-advisory-av26-1035)

### 1.2 Microsoft October 2026 Patch Tuesday (follow-up)
Counts vary by source: **1,407** (Tenable), **1,411** (BleepingComputer), **1,413** (SecurityWeek, TechRepublic), **1,415** (Petri); roughly 70 to 80 rated critical. One exploited zero-day, **CVE-2026-96951** (CVSS **8.8**), a Windows Shell security feature bypass that defeats Mark of the Web; Microsoft has not said how it was used or who reported it. CISA lists it in KEV (one KEV tracker describes it as command injection; the MSRC description says security feature bypass). Two publicly disclosed bugs: **CVE-2026-92707** (MessagePack-CSharp, DoS via uncontrolled allocation) and a .NET Framework-related bypass (**CVE-2026-108398** per earlier reporting). **Windows 11 24H2 Home and Pro reached end of updates** with this release. Microsoft documentation mentions a temporary mitigation of disabling Administrator protection on affected Remote Desktop hosts via Intune or Group Policy; this lowers a safeguard, so scope it narrowly.
**Action:** Patch user endpoints first (MotW bypass enables phishing delivery), then servers; move any 24H2 Home/Pro devices to a supported version.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/microsoft/microsoft-october-2026-patch-tuesday-fixes-1-411-flaws-3-zero-days/) · [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/microsoft-patches-record-1413-vulnerabilities-including-exploited-windows-zero-day/) · [TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-microsoft-october-2026-patch-tuesday/) · [Windows Forum](https://windowsforum.com/news/october-2026-patch-tuesday-1-400-fixes-exploited-windows-shell-zero-day-and-support-deadlines.448186/)

### 1.3 AhsayCBS exploited flaws, CVE-2026-105133 / CVE-2026-105134 (ONGOING, now in KEV)
Huntress first saw exploitation on **7 Oct** (23:20 UTC), chaining **CVE-2026-105133** (auth bypass, CVSS 4.0 **5.5**) with **CVE-2026-105134** (RCE, CVSS 4.0 **9.3**) to run code as **NT AUTHORITY\SYSTEM**, drop JSP webshells and deploy XMRig miners disguised as Edge executables; **five** organizations were seen targeted by 8 Oct. The patch story shifted: advisories said fixed in **10.3.2**, Huntress first said 10.3.4 was unaffected, but on **10 Oct** Ahsay confirmed **10.3.4.0 did not adequately fix** both CVEs and told partners to install hotfix **10.3.4.45** and reboot (delivered via the authenticated partner portal). Per CISA's 14 Oct notice (as reported in search results), both CVEs are now in KEV.
**Action:** Confirm the running build is **10.3.4.45** or later, not just 10.3.4.0; restrict the management console to trusted networks; hunt for unexpected `.jsp` files in the web directory, processes spawned by the Ahsay service, and Edge-named executables outside normal paths.
Sources: [Huntress](https://www.huntress.com/blog/ahsaycbs-flaws-exploit) · [eSecurity Planet](https://www.esecurityplanet.com/news/news-ahsaycbs-patch-fails-two-exploited-vulnerabilities/) · [BleepingComputer](https://bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/unpatched-ahsaycbs-flaws-exploited-to-deploy-webshells-mine-crypto) · [CISA KEV alert, 14 Oct](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/10/14/cisa-adds-four-known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog)

### 1.4 SonicWall SMA1000 pre-auth SSRF, CVE-2026-102255 (ONGOING, follow-up)
CVSS **10.0**. Affected: **12.4.3-03526** and older, **12.5.0-02952** and older. Fixed: **12.4.3-03670**, **12.5.0-03082** or later; no workaround. Defused Cyber and Previdian saw activity from **8 Oct**; Previdian counted **135** attempts from two US IPs. Success is unconfirmed. SonicWall's advisory still reads "no evidence" of exploitation per the latest coverage I found, and the Canadian Cyber Centre acknowledged public reports on 9 Oct. I found no confirmation of a KEV addition (last checked source dated 9 Oct); the two earlier SMA1000 SSRF flaws from July and September are in KEV. No new development found today.
**Action:** Upgrade; restrict WorkPlace and management interfaces; review logs for unusual OPTIONS requests and access to internal services.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/critical-sonicwall-sma-1000-flaw.html) · [eSecurity Planet](https://www.esecurityplanet.com/threats/news-sonicwall-sma1000-cve-2026-102255-exploitation/) · [Singapore CSA AL-2026-137](https://www.csa.gov.sg/alerts-and-advisories/alerts/al-2026-137/)

### 1.5 IDCF Cloud (SoftBank) ransomware, Japan (DEVELOPING, follow-up)
Attack began about **03:40 on 7 Oct**; **495** companies and local governments affected. Four zones in East Japan Region 1 (tesla, henry, pascal, joule) were hit; IDCF's latest verified assessment (8 to 9 Oct) is that data can only be restored from customers' own backups and that customers should rebuild in a separate environment. Management consoles remain suspended in all regions; no unauthorized access confirmed in the other zones. SoftBank is now the customers' contact point. No recovery completion announced as of the latest coverage (12 to 13 Oct); attacker-circulated numbers are unconfirmed by IDCF, and the entry route and actor are undisclosed.
**Action:** Verify you hold tested backups outside your cloud provider and keep hypervisor and management-plane credentials separate.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/ransomware-attack-disrupts-japans-idcf-cloud-used-by-govt-clients/) · [SDxCentral](https://www.sdxcentral.com/news/softbank-national-cloud-arm-attacked-in-seven-minutes-taking-japan-infrastructure-offline/)
