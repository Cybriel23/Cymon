## 1. Risk Watch

### 1.1 Ivanti Connect Secure chain: CVE-2026-111357 and CVE-2026-118527 added to KEV; 2 Nov deadline (ONGOING)
CISA added two more Ivanti Connect Secure (ICS) flaws to KEV on **30 Oct**. Together with **CVE-2026-104935** (authentication bypass and RCE, patched 13 Oct), they form the chain Volexity has tracked since early October under the name **UTA0576**. That chain gives an unauthenticated attacker root on fully patched appliances.
- **CVE-2026-111357:** a missing-authentication flaw in the `dsinvoked` broker, which trusts DSCache configuration data and so lets an attacker escalate to root. Ivanti rates it **8.8**. It was the unpatched third link until Ivanti's **29 Oct** patch. **KEV deadline: 2 Nov**, and CISA's forensic triage requirements apply.
- **CVE-2026-118527:** predictable identifiers that let an authenticated attacker write files with chosen content to a known location on disk. CVSS **4.3**; **KEV deadline: 13 Nov**.
- **Versions:** CISA lists 25.1.1.2, 25.1.2.2 and 22.8R2.5 "and prior" as affected. Secondary reporting gives **25.1.1.2 / 25.1.3.0 / 25.1.3.1 / 22.8R2.5** as fixed. These lists conflict, so verify against Ivanti's advisory.
- **Playbook after exploitation:** LDAP tools that pull LAPS passwords, BitLocker recovery keys and password-bearing attributes (`/tmp/ml.py`, `/tmp/rq5.py`), Kerberoasting (`/tmp/mk.py`), password spraying (`sp_run.py`), and a renamed `tcpdump` (`/data/.icscap/dsstatmond`) that captures cleartext credentials. The actor also truncates logs selectively and patches libraries only in memory, which is why Ivanti's Integrity Checker Tool **missed** it.

Action:
- Patch now to Ivanti's 29 Oct release for your train.
- Hunt for requests to `/dana-na/auth/url_default/welcome.cgi` whose `X-DSCGI-PATH` header points to `wu.cgi` or `/home/runtime/tmp/<md5>`, for files in `/tmp/` and `/data/.icscap/`, and for traffic to `108.165.147[.]64`.
- Do not treat a clean Integrity Checker result as proof of no compromise; use network and memory forensics.
- If compromise is possible, rotate AD service-account, LAPS and BitLocker secrets.

Sources: [CISA KEV alert (30 Oct)](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/10/30/cisa-adds-two-known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog) · [CISA KEV catalog](https://www.cisa.gov/known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog) · [Volexity](https://www.volexity.com/blog/2026/10/13/a-series-of-unauthenticated-events-chained-exploitation-of-ivanti-connect-secure-zero-days/) · [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/10/14/ivanti-patches-connect-secure-zero-day-after-researchers-confirm-in-the-wild-attacks/5303095)

### 1.2 Trojanized "Claude Code" extension on Cursor's marketplace: SSH backdoor on developer machines (NEW)
Between about **02:27 and 13:35 PT on 29 Oct**, Cursor's extension marketplace served a modified `anthropic.claude-code` extension under Anthropic's publisher identity. THN and BleepingComputer cite version **2.1.313**; Microsoft lists **2.1.312 and 2.1.313**. A 66-line loader added to the top of `extension.js` runs on load without any user action.
- **What it installs:** an SSH key with the comment `svc-telemetry` in `authorized_keys`, a reverse tunnel to `134.209.82[.]225` that retries every minute, and persistence on every platform. It also beacons host details, the Git email and the **names** of secret-bearing environment variables to `hxxp://134.209.82[.]225:8099/beacon`.
- **Scope:** Cursor says at most **5,000** users were exposed. Auto-update may have installed it silently. Open VSX and Microsoft's VS Code Marketplace were not affected.
- **Root cause:** not disclosed. Anthropic says its own systems and employee laptops were not compromised, and that the copy was pushed using access to Cursor's marketplace. Cursor took its marketplace offline, rotated all access keys and had the attacker's server taken down.
- **Persistence and IOCs (Microsoft):**
  - macOS: `~/Library/LaunchAgents/com.apple.sysmond.plist`
  - macOS and Linux: `~/.config/.smt` and `.smtk`, plus an `@reboot` crontab entry on Linux
  - Windows: scheduled task `SysMon` (runs as SYSTEM), `%USERPROFILE%\.config\smt.cmd`, and `Startup\sysmon.cmd`
  - Temporary keys: `.stk_<10 hex>`
  - Key fingerprint: `SHA256:0Z6R+DF64Do2nwQic8a7kkfDLNNk4IiK+2U2Rci4htk`
  - JS entry-point SHA-256: `ecb58cb29078d9646ad1dcdbe51526f2d9cfea06be5fb2ab183e8ecc3ab6bf15`

Action:
- Find Cursor installs that took 2.1.312 or 2.1.313, and preserve evidence before cleaning up.
- Remove only the `svc-telemetry` key and the listed persistence objects. Uninstalling the extension does not remove them.
- Block and hunt for `134.209.82[.]225`.
- Rotate the credentials whose variable names were beaconed. Allowlist extension publishers and add an install delay for new versions.

Sources: [Microsoft Security Blog](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2026/10/30/inside-malicious-claude-code-branded-cursor-extension-persistent-ssh-backdoor/) · [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/malicious-claude-code-extension-on-cursor-planted-ssh-backdoors/) · [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/official-claude-code-vs-code-extension.html) · [The Register (Anthropic statement)](https://www.theregister.com/cyber-crime/2026/10/30/anthropic-that-claude-code-extension-compromise-couldnt-be-us/5306682)

### 1.3 PHP2Shell on PAN-OS: US government firewall-management server rooted; all hotfixes out; Fragnesia deadline today (ONGOING)
**Unit 42** independently verified Dream's tooling and results, and Palo Alto says about **0.02%** of its installed base was impacted, namely devices with internet-facing management interfaces. Dream's new detail:
- **Targets:** **240** targets in the US, Japan, Taiwan and South Korea, with **24** firewalls rooted on **23–24 Oct**, in under 15 minutes each.
- **US government victim:** two of the rooted firewalls belonged to a US government-affiliated environment. The attacker used one of them to root a third server that **centrally managed the organisation's firewalls**, and reached its internal Active Directory domain. Dream could not tell whether follow-on password spraying, backdoor-account creation or SAML key extraction succeeded.
- **The chain:** **CVE-2026-109618/-109619 (CVSS 9.2)** plus **CVE-2026-46300 (Fragnesia)**. Dream cites the actor's use of DeepSeek, Kimi and FOFA as indicators of Chinese involvement.
- **Patch status:** **11.1.7-h12** and the other remaining hotfixes (10.2.7-h39, 10.2.10-h42, 11.2.4-h23, 12.1.4-h13, 12.1.10-h3) were released on 29 Oct, so every supported line now has a fix. Threat IDs **543004 and 97622–97678** (content 9162+) give interim coverage.
- **Wider PHP2Shell picture:** watchTowr saw one successful in-the-wild exploitation (from a German IP), and Wordfence knows of **no confirmed WordPress compromises** despite more than 77,000 requests.
- **Fragnesia:** the federal KEV deadline is **31 Oct**.

Action:
- Patch PAN-OS and Panorama, and keep management interfaces off the internet.
- If management was exposed before you patched, treat Panorama and centralised firewall managers as possibly compromised. Review admin accounts, rotate the SAML signing material and the management certificate, and check AD for new accounts and logons from firewall IPs.
- Patch kernels for CVE-2026-46300.

Sources: [Palo Alto Networks advisory](https://security.paloaltonetworks.com/CVE-2026-109618) · [Infosecurity Magazine](https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/chinese-us-government-chained-php2/) · [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/php2shell-attacks-root-palo-alto-firewalls-but-fail-against-wordpress/)

### 1.4 WordPress core race-condition RCE (7.1.4) and Drupal Commerce Core under active attack (NEW)
**WordPress (GHSA-4w3m-c66w-69mg, CVSS 8.1):**
- **The bug:** WordPress **4.7–7.1.3** unpacks full-package core upgrades, including the installer, into a predictable, web-reachable `wp-content/upgrade` path. An unauthenticated attacker who requests `setup-config.php` during the few seconds the update takes can take over the site.
- **Activity:** Wordfence blocked **62,969** attempts in 24 hours, with some sources polling the same sites for about 14.5 hours to catch the update window. It is fixed in **7.1.4** (28 Oct).

**Drupal Commerce Core (CVE-2026-118539, SA-CONTRIB-2026-254, risk 21/25):**
- **The bug:** an access bypass on routes and entity fields. Drupal says it is **exploited in the wild** and "may financially impact the vendor".
- **Fixed in:** **3.3.13** and **8.x-2.41**.

Action:
- Update WordPress to 7.1.4. Grep logs for PHP requests under `wp-content/upgrade/` and `setup-config.php`, and audit for new admin accounts, plugins and PHP files.
- Update Drupal Commerce Core, then audit orders for inconsistencies such as price or status changes.

Sources: [The Hacker News (WordPress)](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/wordpress-rce-flaw-draws-nearly-63000.html) · [Drupal SA-CONTRIB-2026-254](https://www.drupal.org/sa-contrib-2026-254) · [The Hacker News (Drupal)](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/hackers-actively-exploit-highly.html) · [SecurityWeek (Drupal)](https://www.securityweek.com/drupal-commerce-core-vulnerability-exploited-against-online-stores/)

### 1.5 Linux kernel TCP use-after-free CVE-2026-98432: Ubuntu and RHEL still unpatched (follow-up)
**CVE-2026-98432** is the IPv4 TCP connection-setup race (kernel CNA **9.8**; Red Hat **5.9**). Anthropic built an unpublished remote root exploit for it against an MPTCP listener, in about 53 minutes.
- **Distro status (30 Oct):** Ubuntu **22.04 / 24.04 / 26.04 / 26.10** remain vulnerable, with fixes in progress. **RHEL and Rocky 8–10** have fixes planned. Debian is fixed (6.1.190-1 / 6.12.113-1), and Oracle, Amazon and SUSE have partial fixes.
- **Hidden exposure:** disabling MPTCP alone does **not** mitigate it. **Go 1.24+** services enable MPTCP on Linux listeners by default, so some hosts may expose MPTCP listeners without anyone knowing.
- **Exploitation:** none reported in the wild.

Action:
- Patch where fixes exist.
- On Ubuntu and RHEL hosts that face the internet, restrict exposed TCP services to trusted networks, or use CIQ's interim nftables rule to drop IPv4 TCP packets that carry IP options.

Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/critical-linux-kernel-flaw-allows-remote-root-attacks-on-mptcp-servers/) · [Beazley Security](https://www.beazley.security/alerts-advisories/critical-remote-code-execution-vulnerability-in-the-linux-kernel-tcp-stack)

## 4. Threat Watch
- **Help-desk vishing to AD CS domain compromise (Microsoft, 30 Oct):**
  - **Initial access:** in two unattributed intrusions, callers posing as IT staff over Teams talked users into launching **Quick Assist**. No Teams vulnerability was involved.
  - **Tooling:** portable Bun, Python and Java runtimes staged in `KB`-prefixed folders. Case 1 exfiltrated data with Rclone renamed `SearchApp.exe`.
  - **Case 1 (ESC1):** a misconfigured template let the attacker issue certificates for 11 privileged accounts, followed by **DCSync within seconds**.
  - **Case 2 (ESC8):** PetitPotam was relayed to AD CS Web Enrollment to obtain a DC certificate and then a TGT through PKINIT.
  - **Fixes:** audit ESC1-prone templates, remove or harden Web Enrollment (HTTPS plus EPA, no NTLM), restrict RMM tools and Teams external access, and never exclude privileged accounts from automatic containment.
