## 1. Risk Watch

### 1.1 Cisco ASA/FTD SSL VPN zero-day CVE-2026-76418 — exploited, now in KEV (NEW)
**CVE-2026-76418 (CVSS 8.6, CSCwx26625)** lets an unauthenticated attacker reload Cisco Secure Firewall ASA or FTD with a crafted request to the SSL remote-access VPN login service. Cisco published the advisory on **28 Oct**, and CISA added the flaw to KEV on **29 Oct**.
- **When a device is vulnerable:** `webvpn` is enabled on an interface **and** a tunnel group (including `DefaultWEBVPNGroup`) has `secondary-authentication-server-group` configured. No successful login is needed.
- **Fixed releases:** ASA **9.16.4.104 / 9.18.4.96 / 9.20.4.50 / 9.22.3.200 / 9.23.1.48 / 9.24.1.27**; FTD **7.0.11 / 7.2.13 / 7.4.9 / 7.6.7 / 7.7.14 / 10.0.3 / 10.1.1**.
- **Context:** this is the second ASA/FTD VPN DoS zero-day since August (**CVE-2026-20349**). There is no attribution and no IOCs. Shadowserver counts more than **51,000** exposed ASA SSL VPN endpoints.

Action:
- Upgrade now.
- If you can't, apply Cisco's temporary mitigations. Where `DefaultWEBVPNGroup` does not use secondary authentication, set `no tunnel-group-list enable`. Otherwise remove `secondary-authentication-server-group`, which weakens VPN posture.
- Alert on unexpected ASA/FTD reloads and crashinfo files. Repeated DoS against a perimeter VPN can cover or precede other activity.

Sources: [Cisco advisory](https://sec.cloudapps.cisco.com/security/center/content/CiscoSecurityAdvisory/cisco-sa-asaftd-tunnel-dos-s6mgkb75) · [CISA KEV alert (29 Oct)](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/10/29/cisa-adds-two-known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog) · [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/cisco-warns-of-firewall-zero-day-exploited-to-crash-ssl-vpn-devices/) · [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/attackers-exploit-second-cisco-firewall-vpn-zero-day-since-august/)

### 1.2 GitLab CI regex RCE CVE-2026-97229 (CVSS 10) — in KEV; Alpine Linux targeted (NEW)
**The bug:** an integer overflow in Onigmo, the regex engine built into Ruby, which GitLab uses to compile CI regexes. A crafted `.gitlab-ci.yml` plus a UTF-8 file path pushed to a public project gives code execution as the `git` user. A public PoC (Rapid7) has existed since 13 Oct, and CISA added the flaw to KEV on **29 Oct**.

**Alpine Linux:** a freshly registered account sent crafted CI configurations to Alpine's GitLab on **20–21 Oct**. It crashed workers eight times and also downloaded Alpine's GitLab image, apparently to tune the exploit.
- Alpine's 22 Oct upgrade to 19.3.6 did not fix the server, because the server used standard Ruby rather than GitLab's patched build.
- Alpine rebuilt the server, rotated secrets and found no tampered packages. It says code execution "would leave no trace in the web logs".

**Fixed in:** GitLab **19.4.2+ / 19.3.5+ / 19.2.8+**; 19.5, 19.1, 19.0 and 18.11 are not affected. Ruby **3.3.13 / 3.4.12 / 4.0.9**; Ruby 3.2 is EOL and will not be fixed.

Action:
- Upgrade GitLab, and also update Ruby on source or custom-image installs. Upgrading GitLab alone does not fix those.
- Until then, disable open sign-up and restrict writes to public projects.
- Deploy GitLab's six Sigma rules. Hunt for `git`-owned files in `/tmp`, unexpected Puma/Sidekiq child processes, and changed crontabs or SSH keys.
- If compromise is possible, rotate `gitlab-secrets.json`, CI/CD variables, runner tokens and access tokens.

Sources: [CISA KEV alert (29 Oct)](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/10/29/cisa-adds-two-known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog) · [The Hacker News (Alpine)](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/attacker-tried-to-exploit-alpine-linuxs.html) · [BleepingComputer](https://bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/gitlab-warns-of-max-severity-rce-flaw-in-regex-compiler-engine)

### 1.3 PHP2Shell + Fragnesia: Palo Alto confirms PAN-OS management-interface exploitation; Fragnesia deadline 31 Oct (ONGOING)
**Palo Alto:** its advisory now states "active exploitation has been observed targeting the Palo Alto Networks management interface exposed to the internet". This follows Dream's report of a suspected China-nexus operator rooting **25** servers with **CVE-2026-109618/-109619** chained to the Linux LPE **CVE-2026-46300 (Fragnesia)**.
- **Is PAN-OS the vendor in Dream's report?** Dream still has not named the vendor. ISMG and THN tie the campaign to PAN-OS, but THN notes this is an inference.
- **Exposure:** Palo Alto says fewer than **0.01%** of its installed base expose the management interface, and it has contacted those customers.
- **Fix status:** **11.1.7-h12** is the only supported line still without a fix; its ETA is **30 Oct**. For unfixed versions, Palo Alto suggests interim steps: move management to a data-plane interface with a management profile, replace the inbound management certificate, and decrypt and inspect inbound management traffic.

**Other developments:**
- **Fragnesia KEV deadline:** **31 Oct** for federal civilian agencies.
- **Honeypot RCE:** Perplexity's Adel Karimi recorded in-the-wild RCE against Apache mod_php on **PHP 8.4.26**. Fixed PHP releases are **8.5.12 / 8.4.27 / 8.3.36 / 8.2.35**.
- **Retraction:** Unit 42 has retracted its earlier claim that a European government intrusion began with PHP2Shell. Treat reposts of that story with caution.
- **Unconfirmed DPRK claim:** CYFIRMA's "suspected DPRK" PHP2Shell scanning claim is only moderate-confidence, and no compromises have been confirmed.

Action:
- Take PAN-OS and Panorama management interfaces off the internet now, or limit them to a single jump host. Install 11.1.7-h12 as soon as it ships.
- Patch kernels for CVE-2026-46300.
- Hunt on Linux appliances for small PHP webshells, `pam_permit.so` in `/etc/pam.d/su`, reverse SSH/SOCKS tunnels and the `iox` binary. See yesterday's briefing for the full list.

### 1.4 VMware ESX CVE-2026-41709 was a Warp Panda zero-day for audit-trail evasion (follow-up)
**CVE-2026-41709** is the "low-severity" ESX insufficient-logging flaw that CISA added to KEV on 28 Oct. CrowdStrike says China-nexus **Warp Panda** (the BrickStorm operators) used it as a zero-day in intrusions at US organisations, including law firms. The aim was to run admin operations on ESX without audit records.
- **Playbook:** initial access through edge devices, then a pivot to vCenter. Implants include **BrickStorm, TetanusStorm, SharpStorm, DualGauge and GuestConduit**.
- **Related flaws:** the same July Broadcom batch included vCenter **CVE-2026-59309/-59310**, which were mass-exploited in August–September.

Action:
- Update to **ESXi80U3j-25429389+ / ESXi-9.0.2.0100-25595025 / ESXi-9.1.0.0-25370933**.
- Forward ESX and vCenter logs off-host, and look for gaps in hypervisor audit trails.
- Hunt for BrickStorm-family implants on vCenter and appliances.

Sources: [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/chinese-hackers-quietly-used-low-severity-esxi-logging-flaw-as-zero-day/) · [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/china-linked-hackers-exploited-vmware.html)

### 1.5 DebugBear RUM script compromise drains Polymarket users — third-party JavaScript risk (NEW)
Attackers compromised the website-monitoring provider **DebugBear**. They used a file on its CDN, loaded through its Real User Monitoring (RUM) embed, to serve crypto-draining JavaScript.
- **Polymarket (29 Oct):** the script checked whether visitors were logged in and abused their trading sessions to sell positions or move tokens. It used `data.debugbear.com` for C2.
- **Losses and response:** about **$2.5M** was traced to the attacker's wallets. Polymarket halted trading for about an hour and says it will reimburse users.
- **Wider exposure:** DebugBear confirmed an ongoing incident and told customers to remove its embed. Any site that loads the RUM snippet may have served the script.

Action:
- Remove DebugBear RUM embeds until DebugBear gives the all-clear.
- Search web properties and CSP reports for `debugbear.com` loads.
- Enforce CSP and SRI on third-party scripts, especially on authenticated or financial pages.

Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/polymarket-halts-trading-after-crypto-draining-script-breach-25m-traced/) · [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/382185/polymarket-pauses-trading-prediction-market-security-incident)
