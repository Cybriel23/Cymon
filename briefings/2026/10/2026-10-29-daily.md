## 1. Risk Watch

### 1.1 PHP2Shell + Fragnesia: firewalls rooted, KEV deadlines 29 and 31 Oct (ONGOING, escalated)
Dream reports that an operator used FOFA to find about **19,000** internet-facing firewalls from one large vendor (name withheld), narrowed them to about 240 in the US, Japan, Taiwan and South Korea, and exploited **25** on **23–24 Oct**. The chain:
- **Entry:** PHP2Shell (**CVE-2026-109618 / -109619**) gave initial access.
- **Root:** **CVE-2026-46300 "Fragnesia"**, an XFRM ESP-in-TCP local privilege escalation, raised that to root. Fragnesia has had a public PoC since May, and the earlier Dirty Frag patches do not fix it.
- **Government victim:** at the US government-affiliated victim, the attacker opened a reverse SSH tunnel to an internal firewall management server, decrypted an LDAP service-account password, sprayed passwords and tried to create AD backdoor accounts.
- **Attribution:** China-nexus is Dream's assessment, based on FOFA use, the `iox` tunnelling tool, DeepSeek/Kimi configured in an "aiscan" platform, and UTC+8 timestamps. Those artifacts are circumstantial.

CISA added Fragnesia to KEV on **28 Oct**, due **31 Oct**. The PHP2Shell KEV deadline is **today**.

Vendor exposure:
- **Palo Alto Networks:** PAN-OS/Panorama are affected, with fixes such as **12.2.4-h1, 12.1.10-h3, 11.2.16-h1 and 10.2.18-h13**; **11.1.7-h12** has an ETA of 30 Oct.
- **Zyxel, Schneider Electric (SESB-2026-295-01) and Oracle (Secure Backup):** published statements or bulletins on 28 Oct.
- **Sophos:** reports successful exploitation in customer environments.

Action:
- Patch PHP everywhere, including appliance firmware. Keep appliance management interfaces off the internet until the vendor ships fixed builds.
- Patch kernels for CVE-2026-46300, or blacklist the `esp4`/`esp6` modules where IPsec is not used.
- Hunt on Linux-based appliances for: a ~100-byte PHP webshell in a web-accessible path, `/etc/pam.d/su` changed to `pam_permit.so`, new systemd units or `rc.local` tunnel hooks, multi-port reverse SSH/SOCKS5 tunnels, and the `iox` binary.
- Rotate LDAP/AD service credentials stored on firewall management servers.

Sources: [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/china-linked-hackers-exploit-php2shell-to-root-firewalls/) · [Dream](https://dreamgroup.com/blog/chained-php2shell-exploit-roots-25-servers) · [CISA KEV alert (28 Oct)](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/10/28/cisa-adds-two-known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog) · [Tenable (Fragnesia FAQ)](https://www.tenable.com/blog/fragnesia-cve-2026-46300-faq-about-new-linux-kernel-xfrm-esp-in-tcp-priv-esc) · [Palo Alto Networks advisories](https://security.paloaltonetworks.com/) · [Zyxel](https://www.zyxel.com/global/en/support/security-advisories) · [Sophos](https://www.sophos.com/en-us/security-advisories/sophos-sa-20261021-php2shell) · [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/php2shell-attack-roots-25-servers.html)

### 1.2 Node2Shell / Brotli CVE-2026-111497 and Adobe Commerce "PolyShell" CVE-2026-48356 — both now in KEV (NEW)
CISA added the **Brotli decoder out-of-bounds write (CVE-2026-111497, CVSS 4.0 9.2)** to KEV on **27 Oct**, due **10 Nov**.
- **Exploitation:** ELLIO captured an in-the-wild attempt against **Node.js 24.22.0**; it did not produce a shell in lab replay. Imperva has since demonstrated working RCE on a Node.js build.
- **Fixed versions:** Node.js **22.23.4 / 24.22.1 / 26.12.1**; Brotli **1.0.10 / 1.1.1 / 1.2.1**.
- **Wider impact:** Microsoft shipped emergency **.NET** updates, PAN-OS is affected (fixed builds in the same hotfix lines as 1.1), and AWS says other vendors' fixes are still incomplete.

**PolyShell (CVE-2026-48356, CVSS 9.3)** is an unauthenticated file upload in the Magento/Adobe Commerce REST API, fixed in **APSB26-73** (July). Sansec links it to compromises of hundreds of stores, with loaders injected through CMS blocks.

Action:
- Update Node.js, .NET and Python Brotli bindings, and look for vendored copies of Brotli in other software. Restart processes and rebuild container images after patching.
- If you can't patch yet, refuse Brotli-encoded request bodies at the proxy.
- For Magento: apply APSB26-73, then check the upload directories, `cms_block`/`cms_page` content and admin users for planted artifacts. Patching does not remove an attacker who is already in.

Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/cisa-warns-of-hackers-now-exploiting-critical-node2shell-flaw/) · [CISA KEV alert (27 Oct)](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/10/27/cisa-adds-two-known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog) · [Tenable](https://www.tenable.com/blog/brotli2shell-frequently-asked-questions-for-google-brotli-decoder-cve-2026-111497) · [AWS bulletin 2026-155](https://aws.amazon.com/security/security-bulletins/2026-155-aws/) · [The Hacker News (PolyShell)](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/adobe-commerce-polyshell-attacks-breach.html) · [Sysdig detection](https://www.sysdig.com/blog/node2shell-cve-2026-111497-detecting-the-node-js-brotli-rce)

### 1.3 Volt Typhoon "Silent Train" and UAT-11473 "KeyDrift" — China-nexus actors living in management planes (NEW)
**Silent Train (Lumen):** Black Lotus Labs has tracked Silent Train since late 2023 and says it has now "quietly disrupted" it.
- **How it works:** a relay network that runs traffic from a VPS outside the US, through non-US and then US-based hijacked SOHO/IoT devices (Intelbras modems, Netgear Orbi), to the victim. Chains reach up to six hops, and artifacts are cleaned up after each session.
- **Zero-day campaigns:** Versa Director **CVE-2024-39717**, N-able N-central **CVE-2025-8875/8876**, Cisco Catalyst SD-WAN **CVE-2026-20127**, VeloCloud Orchestrator **CVE-2026-16812**, and likely Quest KACE.
- **Victims:** telecoms, government, defence, satellite providers, MSPs and critical infrastructure, focused on **Hawaii, California and Texas**.

**KeyDrift (Talos, 28 Oct):** **UAT-11473**, whose infrastructure overlaps with Silent Train (high confidence), exploited **CVE-2026-20127** and implanted SD-WAN controllers with **KeyDrift**.
- **PolyFlame:** injected into `vdaemon`; provides a TinyShell-style backdoor and relay C2.
- **LittleFilch:** hooks `SSL_read` to steal OMP AES-256 keying material.
- **Targets:** space/satellite, defence-aerospace and policy organisations.

Action:
- Patch Catalyst SD-WAN, VeloCloud, Versa and N-central. Restrict their admin interfaces to management networks.
- On SD-WAN controllers, look for `/tmp/system-vdaemon`, `/var/tmp/vdaemon.bin|.conf`, unexpected `libdssl.so`/`libcommon.so`, a `conf_sshd` file, modified runit scripts under `/etc/sv/sshd/` and `/etc/sv/aaamgr/`, and remounts of `/rootfs.rw`. Deploy Snort SIDs 65938/65958 and 67387–67416.
- Extend log retention on management platforms and alert on out-of-hours admin logins.

Sources: [Lumen Black Lotus Labs](https://www.lumen.com/blog/en-us/silent-train-volt-typhoon-operational-framework) · [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/volt-typhoon-hides-zero-day-attacks-using-silent-train-framework/) · [Cisco Talos](https://blog.talosintelligence.com/uat-11473-keydrift/) · [CyberScoop](https://cyberscoop.com/volt-typhoon-silent-train-covert-ephemeral-network-black-lotus-labs/)

### 1.4 Oracle: E-Business Suite CVE-2026-101767 probed; PeopleSoft CVE-2026-35273 is three chained flaws (NEW / follow-up)
**E-Business Suite:** **CVE-2026-101767** is an unauthenticated SQL injection in EBS's Thin Client Framework servlet that chains to RCE. It was found by Horizon3 and fixed in the **October 2026 CPU** (20 Oct). Defused's decoys recorded exploratory exploitation on **27 Oct**. Shadowserver counts about 670 exposed EBS instances.

**PeopleSoft:** ZDI says the bug that ShinyHunters (UNC6240) has exploited since May (**CVE-2026-35273**) is actually three flaws:
- an SSRF in PSIGW;
- path control in the PSEMHUB `ExecuteProcessActivityCommand` handler, which allows web-shell writes;
- XML deserialisation that fires when PSEMHUB initialises.

The June patch's `validateClient` check still trusts localhost, so PSEMHUB stays reachable through the SSRF. Mandiant reported web shells on dozens of systems by late September.

Action:
- Apply the October CPU to EBS now and remove EBS from direct internet exposure.
- For PeopleSoft: block `/PSEMHUB/` and `/PSIGW/` externally (normalise URL encoding before matching), hunt for web shells and planted XML under PSEMHUB, and watch for Oracle's split CVEs and any follow-up fix.

Sources: [The Register](https://theregister.com/security/2026/10/28/oracle-celebrates-end-of-cybersecurity-awareness-month-with-quadruple-trouble/5306121) · [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-now-scanning-systems-for-recently-patched-oracle-flaw/) · [The Hacker News (PeopleSoft)](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/shinyhunters-exploited-oracle.html) · [The Record (Mandiant)](https://therecord.media/shinyhunters-cyberattacks-oracle-mandiant)

### 1.5 Tailscale "Tailworm" — fixes confirmed in 1.104.4; headscale still exposed (follow-up)
Tailscale's own bulletin feed now confirms four bulletins dated **26 Oct**, all fixed in **1.104.4+**:
- **TS-2026-014:** SSH Unix-socket forwarding reaches root-only sockets such as Docker's. Workaround: `TS_SSH_DISABLE_FORWARDING=1`.
- **TS-2026-015:** SSH session memory exhaustion.
- **TS-2026-016:** serve Unix backends could reach privileged sockets, including the tailscaled LocalAPI (node takeover).
- **TS-2026-017:** an HTTP/2 serve race leaked App-Capabilities headers across mounts.

No headscale fix was found.

Action:
- Upgrade every node to ≥1.104.4 and verify with `tailscale version`.
- On headscale, treat every SSH grant as root on the target and narrow grants to explicit source→destination pairs.

Sources: [Tailscale security bulletins](https://tailscale.com/security-bulletins) · [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/tailscale-patches-ssh-flaws-that-could.html)
