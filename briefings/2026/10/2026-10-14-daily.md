### 1.2 SonicWall SMA1000 pre-auth SSRF, CVE-2026-102255 (ONGOING, follow-up)
CVSS **10.0** (vendor score; NVD analysis pending). Affected: SMA **6210, 7210, 8200v** on **12.4.3-03526** and older or **12.5.0-02952** and older. Fixed: **12.4.3-03670**, **12.5.0-03082** or later; no workaround. Previdian and Defused Cyber first saw activity on **8 Oct**, two days after the 6 Oct patch, and by **12 Oct** Previdian had logged **135** attempts from two US IPs. No successful compromise is confirmed; SonicWall had not updated its advisory and one check on 9 Oct showed the CVE absent from KEV (possibly stale). Earlier SMA1000 flaws were exploited and are in KEV.
**Action:** Upgrade now; restrict WorkPlace and management interfaces; review logs for unusual OPTIONS requests and access to internal services (CouchDB).
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/critical-sonicwall-sma-1000-flaw.html) · [eSecurity Planet](https://www.esecurityplanet.com/threats/news-sonicwall-sma1000-cve-2026-102255-exploitation/) · [BleepingComputer](https://bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/max-severity-sonicwall-sma1000-flaw-now-exploited-in-attacks)

### 1.3 GhostAction GitHub Actions credential theft, new wave (NEW/ONGOING)
Socket reports a burst on **8 Oct** in which accounts (named as henrywoo and kitao) pushed `.github/workflows/security-audit.yml` to every repo they could write to (**346** repos in that burst). The new variant searches the working tree and full git history for cloud credentials, not only Actions secrets, and posts them in cleartext to a hardcoded IP. GitGuardian documents **772** public repos affected between 31 Aug and 30 Sep. Secondary coverage claims **500+** accounts; treat as unverified. The 2025 GhostAction wave stole **3,325** secrets from 817 repos.
**Action:** Search org repos and git history for `security-audit.yml` and unexpected workflows; rotate all Actions secrets and any cloud keys in repo history; review recent commits by compromised or unfamiliar accounts; restrict workflow permissions.
Sources: [Socket](https://www.socket.dev/blog/ghostaction-cloud-credentials) · [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/github-workflows-attack-affects-hundreds-of-repos-thousands-of-secrets/amp/) · [StepSecurity](https://www.stepsecurity.io/blog/ghostaction-campaign-over-3-000-secrets-stolen-through-malicious-github-workflows)

### 1.4 IDCF Cloud (SoftBank) ransomware, Japan (DEVELOPING, follow-up)
Attack began **7 Oct**; **495** companies and local governments affected. East Japan Region 1 was shut down and customer console access disabled in all regions pending checks. Data in four zones may be unrecoverable and IDCF advised customers to rebuild on a separate environment from their own backups. No recovery announced as of 12 to 13 Oct; entry route and actor remain undisclosed. Downstream: JR East says up to **6.09M** records may have been accessed (running total, possible double counting).
**Action:** Confirm backups exist outside your cloud provider and that restores are tested; separate hypervisor and management-plane credentials.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/ransomware-attack-disrupts-japans-idcf-cloud-used-by-govt-clients/) · [SDxCentral](https://www.sdxcentral.com/news/softbank-national-cloud-arm-attacked-in-seven-minutes-taking-japan-infrastructure-offline/) · [Cybernews](https://cybernews.com/security/japanese-cloud-cyberattack-railways-police-495-organizations-idcf/)

### 1.5 Fortinet FortiMail CVE-2026-104286 and Citrix NetScaler CVE-2026-88771/-88772/-88779 (ONGOING, follow-up)
FortiMail (**FG-IR-26-175**, CVSS **9.8**, KEV since 1 Oct): named fixes **7.4.9, 7.6.7, 8.0.2**; **7.2** has no fix; I still cannot confirm the builds on Fortinet's own page. NetScaler (**CTX697096**): two exploited RCEs (CVSS **9.5**) plus SAML flaw **CVE-2026-88779** (CVSS **8.7**, KEV 4 Oct); fixed **14.1-73.41**, **13.1-64.28**. No new developments found today.
**Action:** FortiMail: verify build, upgrade, hunt for new files in web-served directories and requests with `%00` or `../`. NetScaler: capture memory and disk before patching or rebooting, hunt webshells and new admin accounts, rotate reachable secrets.
Sources: [Singapore CSA AL-2026-133](https://www.csa.gov.sg/alerts-and-advisories/alerts/al-2026-133/) · [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/critical-fortimail-zero-day-flaw.html) · [CISA](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/09/27/critical-zero-day-vulnerabilities-exploited-citrix-netscaler-adc-gateway)
