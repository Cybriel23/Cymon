## 1. Risk Watch

### 1.1 Palo Alto PAN-OS management interface RCE, CVE-2026-0322 (NEW, EXPLOITED)
Disclosed **14 Oct**; Palo Alto reports very limited exploitation against the management interface. Code injection (CWE-94), CVSS 4.0 **8.6**. Per secondary sources it needs valid high-privilege credentials plus network reach to the management interface, and then yields code execution as **root**. Affects PA-Series, VM-Series and Panorama; Cloud NGFW and Prisma Access are not affected. CISA added it to KEV (14 Oct alert) with a **17 Oct** deadline and forensic-triage language under BOD 26-04. Reported fixed builds include **12.2.4**, **12.1.4-h12 / 12.1.7-h8 / 12.1.10-h2**, **11.1.x** hotfix trains and **10.2.x** hotfix trains; the 11.2 fix is inconsistently reported (**11.2.16**, possibly not yet shipped), so confirm against the advisory. Sources disagree on workarounds; restricting management access is the practical control. Because credentials are required, exploitation implies prior credential compromise or a chained flaw, which makes this a likely second-stage tool.
**Action:** Patch per the Palo Alto advisory; confine the management interface to a dedicated management network; rotate admin credentials and review admin login and config-change logs back to at least early October; hunt for new local users, unexpected processes and outbound connections from the firewall.
Sources: [Qualys ThreatPROTECT](https://threatprotect.qualys.com/2026/10/15/palo-alto-networks-pan-os-remote-code-execution-vulnerability-exploited-in-attack-cve-2026-0322/) · [HOL guide](https://hol.org/blog/cve-2026-0322-pan-os-management-interface-rce-kev) · [CISA KEV alert, 14 Oct](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/10/14/cisa-adds-four-known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog)

### 1.2 Ivanti Connect Secure chain, CVE-2026-104935 (ONGOING, follow-up)
Volexity attributes the chain to **UTA0576**, active since about **3 Oct** on appliances running the then-latest 22.8R2.4. Two flaws (auth bypass and RCE, tracked together as CVE-2026-104935) are fixed; the **privilege-escalation link is still unpatched**, with Ivanti promising a fix "in a future release in the coming weeks". The exploit lives in memory and restores altered config, so the **Integrity Checker Tool may not detect it**. Affected ranges differ by source (reports cite 22.8R2.4 and earlier, 25.1.2.2 and earlier, and "before 25.1.3.0"; 22.8R2.5 and 25.1.3.0/25.1.3.1 are listed as unaffected by one outlet). I found no new Ivanti release today. The KEV due date reported for this entry is **16 Oct**.
**Action:** Upgrade to a build the advisory lists as fixed; restrict internet exposure of the appliance; treat any pre-patch exposure as potential compromise, preserve memory and network forensics, rotate credentials and certificates stored on the device, and watch for the escalation fix.
Sources: [iTnews](https://itnews.com.au/news/attackers-chain-three-ivanti-vpn-zero-days-for-root-access-629621) · [BleepingComputer](https://bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/ivanti-warns-of-connect-secure-zero-day-exploited-in-attacks) · [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/attacker-chains-three-ivanti-connect.html)

### 1.3 Atlassian Data Center file-access flaw, CVE-2026-21589 (NEW to KEV, EXPLOITED)
Unauthenticated arbitrary file access in self-hosted **Jira, Confluence and Bitbucket** (Atlassian rates it **9.3**; reporting says eight products are affected; Cloud is already patched). watchTowr published details on **6 Oct** (root cause: routing code that converts double colons to forward slashes, bypassing path restrictions) and exploitation followed within hours; one tracker counted **158** attempts from **26** IPs in eight countries by 7 Oct. Atlassian says an attacker needs the exact path and cannot list directories, but watchTowr showed it can expose a **Crowd password** leading to Jira admin. CISA added it to KEV on **15 Oct**.
**Action:** Patch self-hosted Data Center instances or apply Atlassian's temporary mitigations; rotate secrets in config files reachable from the app (Crowd, database, SMTP, API tokens); review web logs for requests containing `::` path sequences since 6 Oct.
Sources: [CISA KEV alert, 15 Oct](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/10/15/cisa-adds-one-known-exploited-vulnerability-catalog) · [BleepingComputer](https://bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-exploit-critical-atlassian-flaw-after-public-poc-release) · [Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/?p=387226) · [eSecurity Planet](https://www.esecurityplanet.com/news/news-atlassian-cve-2026-21589-file-read-flaw/)

### 1.4 European supermarket refrigeration cyberattacks (DEVELOPING)
French Finance Minister Roland Lescure told Parliament about **700 of roughly 10,000** French supermarkets were affected (reported 13 Oct); Carrefour, Monoprix and **300** Intermarché/Netto stores (Groupement Mousquetaires) are named. Danfoss, the refrigeration-management software supplier, said most affected systems were **older products no longer receiving security updates**. BFM-TV reports attackers forced continuous defrost cycles and set temperatures as high as 50 °C; spoiled food and some fires were reported. The Paris prosecutor's cybercrime unit is investigating. No group has claimed it or been attributed. Separately, **Ahold Delhaize** took systems offline after a "security issue"; brand websites (Stop & Shop, Food Lion, Albert Heijn) were still down on 13 Oct, stores stayed open, and the company reports no evidence of customer or employee data theft. These are not shown to be the same incident.
**Action:** Inventory internet-reachable building-management and refrigeration controllers, remove them from direct exposure, apply vendor updates or isolate end-of-life units, and check for default or shared credentials.
Sources: [AP via ABC News](https://abcnews.com/amp/Business/wireStory/cyberattack-targeting-refrigeration-systems-hits-700-supermarkets-french-137230099) · [PBS NewsHour](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/cyberattacks-disrupt-refrigeration-systems-at-european-supermarkets)

### 1.5 SonicWall SMA1000 CVE-2026-102255 and IDCF Cloud (ONGOING, follow-ups)
**SonicWall:** CVSS **10.0** pre-auth SSRF in Appliance WorkPlace (SMA 6210, 7210, 8200v). Fixed in **12.4.3-03670** and **12.5.0-03082**. Defused Cyber and Previdian saw attempts from **8 Oct** (**135** attempts from two US IPs); success is not established, SonicWall's advisory still has not been updated to confirm exploitation, and SOCRadar notes the evidence does not show independent RCE or credential theft. No KEV entry found. **IDCF Cloud (SoftBank), Japan:** attack began **7 Oct**, **495** organisations affected; the provider's latest verified view (8 to 9 Oct) is that data in the four hit East Japan Region 1 zones is recoverable only from customers' own backups. As of 12 Oct no recovery completion had been announced, I found nothing newer, and root cause and actor remain undisclosed.
**Action:** SonicWall: upgrade and restrict WorkPlace exposure. IDCF customers: rebuild from offline backups; everyone: keep backups and hypervisor credentials outside the provider's management plane.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/max-severity-sonicwall-sma1000-flaw-now-exploited-in-attacks) · [SecurityWeek](https://securityweek.com/critical-sonicwall-vulnerability-exploited-days-after-disclosure) · [SDxCentral](https://www.sdxcentral.com/news/softbank-national-cloud-arm-attacked-in-seven-minutes-taking-japan-infrastructure-offline/) · [BleepingComputer on IDCF](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/ransomware-attack-disrupts-japans-idcf-cloud-used-by-govt-clients/)
