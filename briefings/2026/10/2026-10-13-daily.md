Tempo is moderate and dominated by edge-device exploitation and the fallout from the IDCF Cloud ransomware attack. Four items matter most today. Caveat: most sources were reached through search summaries, so verify CVE details and fixed versions against vendor advisories before acting. Watch this week for: Patch Tuesday zero-days and CISA KEV additions that follow, a SonicWall/CISA exploitation confirmation, Fortinet FortiMail build confirmation, the Bitget vendor being named, and IDCF intrusion-path findings.

### 1.2 SonicWall SMA1000 pre-auth SSRF, CVE-2026-102255 (NEW)
CVSS **10.0** SSRF in the Appliance WorkPlace interface of SMA **6210, 7210, 8200v**. Affected: **12.4.3-03526** and older, **12.5.0-02952** and older (platform-hotfix builds). Fixed: **12.4.3-03670** and **12.5.0-03082** or later. A researcher's honeypots saw exploitation attempts (reportedly crafted OPTIONS requests reaching internal CouchDB); whether any succeeded is unclear. SonicWall says it has no evidence of exploitation, and the flaw was not in KEV as of 9 Oct. Earlier SMA1000 SSRF flaws from July and September were exploited and are in KEV. Shadowserver sees **400+** exposed appliances.
**Action:** Upgrade now; restrict the management and WorkPlace interfaces; review logs for unusual OPTIONS requests and internal service access.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/max-severity-sonicwall-sma1000-flaw-now-exploited-in-attacks) · [Singapore CSA AL-2026-137](https://www.csa.gov.sg/alerts-and-advisories/alerts/al-2026-137/) · [Rescana](https://www.rescana.com/post/sonicwall-sma1000-cve-2026-102255-preauth-ssrf)

### 1.3 Fortinet FortiMail unauthenticated file write, CVE-2026-104286 (ONGOING, follow-up)
Advisory **FG-IR-26-175**, CVSS **9.8**, exploited and on KEV since 1 Oct. Affected: **8.0.0 to 8.0.1, 7.6.0 to 7.6.6, 7.4.0 to 7.4.8**. Named fixes **7.4.9, 7.6.7, 8.0.2**; I still could not confirm release on Fortinet's own page (Singapore CSA says released). **7.2** has no fix. Interim mitigation: disable IBE support or restrict management access.
**Action:** Verify the build on the advisory and upgrade; hunt for new files in web-served directories and requests containing `%00` or `../`.
Sources: [Singapore CSA AL-2026-133](https://www.csa.gov.sg/alerts-and-advisories/alerts/al-2026-133/) · [Field Effect](https://fieldeffect.com/blog/patches-pending-actively-exploited-fortimail-flaw) · [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/critical-fortimail-zero-day-flaw.html)

### 1.4 Citrix NetScaler CVE-2026-88771 / -88772 / -88779 (ONGOING, follow-up)
Bulletin **CTX697096**: two exploited unauthenticated RCEs (CVSS **9.5**) plus SAML flaw **CVE-2026-88779** (CVSS **8.7**). Fixed: **14.1-73.41**, **13.1-64.28**. CISA advised checking for compromise before patching or rebooting. No new developments found today.
**Action:** Capture memory and disk before patching; hunt for webshells and new admin accounts; rotate secrets reachable from the appliance.
Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/citrix-admins-warned-to-shut-down-netscalers-over-2-exploited-zero-days/) · [CISA](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/09/27/critical-zero-day-vulnerabilities-exploited-citrix-netscaler-adc-gateway)

### 1.5 IDCF Cloud (SoftBank) ransomware, Japan (DEVELOPING, follow-up)
Ransomware hit IDCF Cloud on **7 Oct**; **495** organizations affected and data in four East Japan zones (tesla, henry, pascal, joule) may never be recovered. New: JR East and View Card say up to **6.09M** records may have been accessed via email-delivery services on IDCF (a running total that may double-count people), and JR Kyushu about **1.3M** emails; they say card numbers, addresses and phone numbers were not exposed. The attacker is unidentified, the entry route is unknown, and attacker-claimed figures (e.g. 554,153 deleted snapshots) are unverified.
**Action:** Verify backups are held outside your cloud provider and test restores; segregate hypervisor and management-plane credentials.
Sources: [Cybernews](https://cybernews.com/security/japanese-cloud-cyberattack-railways-police-495-organizations-idcf/) · [Japan Times](https://www.japantimes.co.jp/business/2026/10/10/tech/japanese-firms-data-breaches/) · [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/ransomware-attack-disrupts-japans-idcf-cloud-used-by-govt-clients/)
