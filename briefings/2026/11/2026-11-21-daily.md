# Daily Cyber Threat Intelligence Briefing
Saturday, 21 November 2026 · Coverage: 20 Nov 2026 06:00 UTC – 21 Nov 2026 06:00 UTC and follow-ups on tracked topics

## Executive Summary
Tempo is still high going into the weekend, and CISA has set a hard short-term deadline. Four concerns lead today. First, CISA added four exploited flaws to KEV with a **23 Nov (Monday)** due date: the **vCenter CVE-2026-123890** zero-day (ARMORY CASTLE), **Cisco Expressway CVE-2026-76466**, **Apache Roller CVE-2026-129448** and **SAP NetWeaver CVE-2025-42884**. Treat that as weekend work for any exposed instances. Second, F5 shipped out-of-band hotfixes for **five critical unauthenticated RCE flaws in BIG-IP APM and iRules**. F5 offers no mitigations, and more than 14,600 APM endpoints are exposed online. Third, SonicWall says **Gen 6 firewalls will get no further patches, even for critical flaws**, and expects attackers to target internet-facing units. Fourth, **Java4Shell** is still not confirmed as exploited, but probing continues and some vendor builds are lagging. Separately, the week's run of US outages has started a debate about patch velocity: officials tie the NSA network disruption to PHP patching. Incident news is dominated by government and enterprise breaches: **USDA** farm-payment systems are offline, the **West Virginia Legislature** is hit, and **Bell Canada** reports an "agentic" intruder. Amazon's **Cairn** report documents AI agents finding and exploiting a zero-day on their own. Watch this week for: KEV remediation deadlines on 23 Nov, F5 and NGINX exploit attempts, fixed Tomcat 11.0.30 / 10.1.63 / 9.0.125 releases, vendor builds for Java4Shell (Cisco, Atlassian, Elastic), a possible Microsoft fix for FastUnlock, and more n0n-linked third-party disclosures.

## 1. Risk Watch

### 1.1 KEV: vCenter CVE-2026-123890 and Cisco Expressway CVE-2026-76466 due Monday 23 Nov; new Expressway IOCs (follow-up, ONGOING)
CISA added **CVE-2026-123890** (VMware vCenter, CVSS **9.8**, `vmdird` SRP-6a session-key disclosure on port 2012) and **CVE-2026-76466** (Cisco Expressway clustered RCE as root, CVSS **9.8**) on **20 Nov**. Both have an unusually short due date of **23 Nov**. The vCenter flaw is fixed in **9.1.1.0200, 9.0.2.0200 and 8.0 U3m**; **7.0 gets no patch**. GTIG/Mandiant tie it to **ARMORY CASTLE (ex-UNC5221)**, which reached vCenter through BeyondTrust and used Golden SAML. For Expressway, the fix is **X15.5.20**, and X14 and earlier must migrate. F6's write-up says the intrusion compromised **four** nodes: two internet-facing **X15.5.3** devices, then two internal cluster nodes. The attackers then ran AI-assisted Python tooling against SMB, SQL Server, WinRM and GitLab. The activity is not attributed to any group.
**Action:** Patch both before Monday; isolate or retire vCenter 7.0. For vCenter, run the hunt list from yesterday's briefing (VAMI SSH enablement, rogue `*@vsphere.local` admins, `/tmp/123`, `/tmp/sss`, `/usr/bin/vmtoolscs`, Slack API egress). For Expressway, block TCP **4371–4372** except between cluster peers. Check `erlang.log` for **`wombat@127.0.0.1`** and look for Python scripts staged under `/mnt/harddisk/i####*/`. IOC IPs: **66.179.31[.]32** and **18.130.47[.]116**. On a hit, rotate domain, SQL and GitLab credentials.
**Sources:** [CISA KEV](https://www.cisa.gov/known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog), [The Hacker News (Expressway IOCs)](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/hackers-exploit-critical-cisco.html), [Help Net Security (vCenter)](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/11/20/vmware-vcenter-zero-day-cve-2026-123890-exploited/), [Dark Reading](https://www.darkreading.com/cyberattacks-data-breaches/china-armory-castle-vmware-vcenter-zero-day)

### 1.2 F5 BIG-IP APM / iRules: five critical unauthenticated RCEs, emergency hotfixes, no mitigations (NEW)
F5 released out-of-band hotfixes (K000164058, with installation guidance in K000164239) for five critical stack-based buffer overflows: **CVE-2026-121805, CVE-2026-120292, CVE-2026-120285, CVE-2026-116953 and CVE-2026-123637**. They affect **BIG-IP Access Policy Manager (APM)** and the **iRules** feature. On vulnerable configurations, an unauthenticated attacker can crash the TMM or execute code. A sixth, high-severity flaw, **CVE-2026-126822**, is an integer underflow in APM SAML that causes DoS and, per F5, "may" allow RCE. It is data-plane only. F5 lists **no mitigations** and reports no exploitation so far. Shadowserver sees about **177,000** BIG-IP instances and **14,600+** IPs with APM fingerprints online. F5 edge devices have a long KEV history (seven entries, four used by ransomware, plus September's APM zero-day CVE-2026-94127), and patch-to-exploit times this month have been very short (see Expressway). Expect weaponization within days.
**Action:** Apply the hotfixes to APM-licensed and iRules-using BIG-IPs now, starting with internet-facing APM portals. Review TMM core dumps and unexpected TMM restarts since mid-November, and watch for unusual SAML assertions arriving at APM. CVSS values and per-branch fixed versions are in F5's per-CVE articles (K000163937, K000163939–42, K000163946).
**Sources:** [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/f5-releases-emergency-patches-for-critical-big-ip-rce-flaws/), [Cyber Security News](https://cybersecuritynews.com/critical-f5-big-ip-vulnerabilities/)

### 1.3 SonicWall Gen 6 firewalls: no more security patches, even for critical flaws (NEW)
SonicWall told customers running **Generation 6** firewalls on **SonicOS 6.x** to retire them immediately and stop exposing them at the internet edge. It warns that "internet-facing Gen 6 firewalls [will] be actively targeted" as attackers use automation and AI to find and exploit unsupported devices. Named models: **TZ300P/TZ350/TZ350W, TZ500/TZ500W, SOHO/SOHO W, SOHO 250/250W and NSa 9250/9450/9650**. Gen 6 SSL VPN has been a repeated ransomware initial-access vector (Akira and Fog), and future SonicOS 6 bugs will stay unpatched.
**Action:** Inventory Gen 6 units and prioritize those that are internet-facing or provide remote access for replacement with Gen 8 / SonicOS 8. Until then: disable WAN management (HTTPS/SSH/SNMP), disable SSL VPN or restrict it to known source IPs, enforce MFA, reset local passwords, and alert on configuration changes and unusual logins.
**Sources:** [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/sonicwall-warns-gen-6-firewalls-wont.html)

### 1.4 Java4Shell (CVE-2026-114938): no confirmed exploitation, probing continues, vendor builds lag (follow-up, ONGOING)
Status is unchanged in substance. GreyNoise sees exploit-shaped requests but no success against its sensors. VulnCheck (for customers) and Immersive's Kevin Breen hold working RCE exploits privately. Public work has reproduced crashes and a memory leak but not RCE. OX Security estimates under **0.12%** of JVMs meet the RCE preconditions, but the crash path is easy, so DoS is the realistic near-term risk. OpenJDK fixes are **17.0.21.1, 21.0.13.1, 25.0.5.1 and 27.0.1.1**. Quarkus says the UBI Java images were fixed on **19 Nov**. Cycode reported some distribution builds (Temurin 17 among them) still pending when it checked. The CVE is still **not in KEV**. Azul's CTO warns that rushing large non-security-only JDK updates into production is itself causing outages, so prefer security-only builds where they are available.
**Action:** Patch internet-facing JVMs first and confirm the *running* JDK version. Where a fix is not yet available, use `-XX:-UseBASE64Intrinsics` if the vendor supports it. Alert on repeated `hs_err_pid*.log` crash dumps and `java4shell-recon` User-Agents. Track the Cisco, Atlassian Data Center and Elastic fixed builds.
**Sources:** [Tenable FAQ](https://www.tenable.com/blog/java4shell-cve-2026-114938-frequently-asked-questions-about-openjdk-rce-vulnerability), [Censys](https://censys.com/advisory/cve-2026-114938/), [Quarkus](https://quarkus.io/blog/java4shell/), [OX Security](https://www.ox.security/blog/java4shell-cve-2026-114938-rce-risk/), [SecurityWeek (Feedback Friday)](https://www.securityweek.com/industry-reactions-to-major-outages-and-ai-patching-questions-feedback-friday/)

## 2. Vulnerability Watch
- **CISA KEV (20 Nov, catalog 2026.11.20, 1,790 entries):** four additions, all due **23 Nov**: **CVE-2026-123890** VMware vCenter, **CVE-2026-76466** Cisco Expressway, **CVE-2026-129448** Apache Roller (deserialization) and **CVE-2025-42884** SAP NetWeaver Enterprise Portal (unauthenticated JNDI injection leading to RCE, patched in SAP's November 2025 Patch Day). The theme is internet-facing middleware and virtualization management. Deadlines due **today, 21 Nov**: **CVE-2026-90436** Apache Axis, **CVE-2026-90817** REDCap and **CVE-2024-10441** Synology DSM. [CISA KEV](https://www.cisa.gov/known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog), [SecurityWeek (Roller/Axis)](https://www.securityweek.com/critical-apache-roller-axis-vulnerabilities-exploited-in-attacks/)
- **OpenSSL CVE-2026-121481 (EVP_EncodeBlock heap overflow):** with very large inputs, the Base64 encoder's output-length calculation overflows and it writes past the caller's buffer. The CVE record lists **4.0.0–4.0.3 and 4.1.0** as affected, with 4.0.4 and 4.1.1 as the upper bounds. It carries a **9.8** CVSS, but Ubuntu rates it **Medium**. Real exposure needs an application that Base64-encodes huge untrusted input, and TLS use alone does not expose a service. Bundled copies (for example Ubuntu 22.04's nodejs) need separate checks. This is a single secondary source, so verify against OpenSSL's vulnerabilities page and your distribution's tracker. [Cyber Security News](https://cybersecuritynews.com/openssl-heap-buffer-overflow/)
- **Apache Tomcat CVE-2026-133325 (critical):** in Tribes clusters that use **TLS 1.3 with a pre-shared key**, Tomcat does not authenticate the peer, so a remote attacker with network access to the cluster port can join the cluster. Affected: **11.0.28–11.0.29, 10.1.62 and 9.0.124**. Fixes are **11.0.30, 10.1.63 and 9.0.125**, which were still pending release when reported. Workaround: switch cluster traffic to TLS 1.2 with the PSK kept, and firewall cluster ports. This is not the KEV-listed **CVE-2026-34486**. Single secondary source; verify against Apache's Tomcat security pages. [Cyber Security News](https://cybersecuritynews.com/critical-apache-tomcat-flaw/)
- **Ruby2Shell (CVE-2026-132825, CVSS 7.4):** a heap overflow in case conversion for single-byte encodings (e.g. `ß` → `SS` writes two bytes where one was checked). Veria Labs says unauthenticated triggering is possible via Rails and classic Sinatra, and that **GitHub Enterprise Server** is affected. GitLab, Mastodon, Discourse, Chatwoot and OpenProject have patched. watchTowr expects DoS rather than reliable RCE. The fixes are in **Ruby 4.0.10 / 3.4.13 / 3.3.14** (same release as yesterday's seven regex bugs). [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/ruby-patches-ruby2shell-vulnerability-that-could-lead-to-rce/)
- **WhatsApp (November advisory):** **CVE-2026-23871** (Android/Business before **2.26.17.4**) lets a crafted Click-to-WhatsApp ad reply leak the victim's IP address. WhatsApp found it while disrupting a scam campaign and believes the scammer abused it. **CVE-2026-102769** is a SQL injection via a crafted "order details" message (before **2.26.35.74**) that can delete local files; it is not exploited. **CVE-2026-44908** is a remote-attestation verifier bypass for the Meta AI confidential-compute backend. [Security Affairs](https://securityaffairs.com/203622/security/cve-2026-23871-whatsapp-bug-helped-scammers-make-messages-look-legitimate.html), [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/whatsapp-fixes-flaw-uncovered-while-disrupting-scam-campaign/)
- **AI-found zero-day in an open-source medical imaging AI app:** Amazon says Cairn agents found an upload-filename path traversal (the advisory points to the **MONAI Label** project). They escalated to root RCE by writing a reverse shell into `sitecustomize.py` and triggering the training API. The maintainers have released a fix and a GHSA. Research deployments of MONAI Label exposed to the internet should update and restrict access. [AWS Security Blog](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/security/chinese-nexus-actors-cairn-ai-agents-found-a-zero-day-on-their-own/)
- **Drupal SA-CORE-2026-017 (CVE-2026-132165) (follow-up):** no in-the-wild exploitation reported yet. Keep `/core/install.php` blocked even on patched sites (**11.4.13 / 11.3.22 / 10.6.22**). [SecurityOnline](https://securityonline.info/drupal-remote-code-execution-cve-2026-132165/)
- **Synology DSM OAuth CVE-2026-120382 (follow-up, unconfirmed):** Synology had promised fixes by 20 Nov, but no source in the coverage window confirms the release. Keep the OAuth Service package disabled until Synology's advisory lists fixed builds. [Synology advisories](https://www.synology.com/security/advisory)
- **Debian CA trust:** more than **100** Debian packages still ship distrusted CA certificates in bundled trust stores despite the ca-certificates updates. Audit applications that carry their own CA bundles. [Cyber Security News](https://cybersecuritynews.com/debian-packages-ca-certificates/)

## 4. Threat Watch
- **Chinese-nexus actor using autonomous Cairn agents (Amazon):** an exposed, unauthenticated Cairn instance (found **10 Sep**) showed a roughly 48-hour campaign starting **8 Sep** against **East Asian universities**. The agents made 148 actions over 16 hours against an open Redis instance, used Qdrant SSRF to map the network, and ran a 30,000-credential spray built from faculty directories. All of these failed. The one success was a self-discovered **zero-day**: API mapping to arbitrary file write took **about 11 minutes**, and a root shell followed. The operator then pointed the agents at a second university through a tunnel. Attribution (moderate confidence) rests on Simplified Chinese prompts and CST timestamps. Cairn joins ARTEX as an open-source agentic framework now used offensively. [AWS Security Blog](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/security/chinese-nexus-actors-cairn-ai-agents-found-a-zero-day-on-their-own/), [The Record](https://therecord.media/amazon-research-cairn-ai-university-attacks-possible-zero-day)
