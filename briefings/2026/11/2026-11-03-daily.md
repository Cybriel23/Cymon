## 1. Risk Watch

### 1.1 Supermarket refrigeration attacks spread to four more European countries (DEVELOPING)
**What happened (overnight 1–2 Nov):** attackers remotely put evaporators into continuous forced-defrost mode with setpoints around **50°C**, spoiling stock.
- **Belgium:** Delhaize (about 40 stores), five Spar franchise stores (via Colruyt) and some Carrefour stores.
- **Luxembourg:** Ahold Delhaize and Colruyt reported incidents.
- **Sweden:** several ICA stores and six Coop stores.
- **Estonia:** an unnamed chain.

**How:** a Swedish consultant says the attackers used valid logins to controllers exposed directly to the internet. Danfoss sits at the centre again, as in the mid-October French wave (hundreds of stores, fires, stock destroyed).

**Exposure (Censys, 2 Nov):** just over **1,000** Danfoss StoreView instances are still exposed worldwide.
- **France:** 220, down from 1,825 before the October attacks.
- **Belgium 138, Netherlands 51, Sweden 50.**
- **Unpatched share:** between **32% and 63%** per country are still below firmware **4.3.1**.

**Attribution:** no group has claimed the attacks. Separately, US officials quoted anonymously suspect **Iran** in freezer outages at about six US military commissaries since late August (covered 31 Oct). No link between the US and European incidents has been established.

Action:
- Take refrigeration and building-management controllers off the public internet and allow access only through a VPN, following CERT-FR's guidance.
- Update StoreView to **4.3.1+**.
- Reset all vendor and integrator credentials.
- Alert on defrost-mode or setpoint changes made outside maintenance windows, especially overnight.
- Retailers and food logistics firms should check outsourced refrigeration contracts for who owns remote access.

### 1.2 Linux kernel CVE-2026-98432: RHEL fixed, Ubuntu still waiting (follow-up)
**Patch status:**
- **Red Hat:** rates the remote IPv4 TCP use-after-free **Critical (CVSS 9.8)** and shipped fixes for **RHEL 8, 9 and 10 on 1 Nov**. Its interim firewall rule (bulletin **RHSB-2026-016**) reduces exposure but "cannot fully prevent" exploitation.
- **Debian, SUSE, Oracle Linux and Amazon Linux:** have fixes for at least some versions.
- **AWS:** updated bulletin **2026-160** on **2 Nov** with managed-patch dates for Bottlerocket, RDS/Aurora, Redshift and Storage Gateway.
- **Arista:** published a related advisory (0203) on 2 Nov.
- **Ubuntu:** the tracker (last updated **30 Oct**) still shows no USN. Fixes are in progress for 22.04, 24.04, 26.04 and 26.10. 20.04, 18.04 and 16.04 are listed as vulnerable with no fix in progress.

**Exploit:** Anthropic's lab exploit finds the needed memory location in under an hour and gets root in about five minutes more. Failed attempts do not crash the target, so attackers can retry. No in-the-wild exploitation or public exploit code is reported.

**Northflank:** its 2 Nov update says it patched CVE-2026-98432 across its fleet as part of incident response (see Incident Watch). It has not said whether the bug played a role in the breach.

Action:
- Apply RHEL kernels now.
- On Ubuntu, prioritise internet-facing Go services (Go 1.24+ opens MPTCP listeners by default) and other exposed TCP listeners. Use the interim mitigations (restrict exposure, drop IPv4 packets carrying IP options) until USNs land.
- Track `pro fix CVE-2026-98432 --dry-run` for Ubuntu Pro hosts.

Sources: [Red Hat CVE page](https://access.redhat.com/security/cve/CVE-2026-98432) · [Ubuntu CVE tracker](https://ubuntu.com/security/CVE-2026-98432) · [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/382375/claude-built-linux-exploit-bug-ubuntu) · [AWS bulletin 2026-160](https://aws.amazon.com/security/security-bulletins/2026-160-aws/) · [Sophos advisory](https://www.sophos.com/en-us/security-advisories/sophos-sa-20261102-linux-kernel-tcp)

### 1.3 Siemens SIMATIC IoT2050 / Node-RED CVE-2026-58115 (CVSS 10.0) exploited in the wild (NEW)
**The flaw:** the Node-RED admin API on SIMATIC IoT2050 Advanced devices running Industrial OS requires no authentication, so anyone who can reach it can deploy flows that run code as root.
- **Fix:** **4.3.4.1** or later (Siemens advisory **SSA-834709**, August).
- **Exploitation (VulnCheck):** canaries caught attacks from **30 Oct**, with 16 events from one source IP through 1 Nov. The same IP also probed Solr, Camunda, Gitea, MLflow, Traefik and Joomla.
- **Payloads:**
  - A bot binary fetched from `85.215.73[.]250:8080` and run from `/dev/shm/.b`.
  - A second stage from `45.194.90[.]57:38472/.sys_update`, saved as `/tmp/.kworker`.
  - Flows that expose an HTTP endpoint (for example `/cmd`) wired to an `exec` node. This backdoor survives reboots.
- **Other vendors:** VulnCheck notes other industrial vendors (for example Emerson) also ship Node-RED, but has not validated whether their products are affected.
- **KEV:** not in CISA KEV yet; VulnCheck has added it to its own KEV list.

Action:
- Patch or uninstall Node-RED. If you must keep it, enable HTTPS and authenticate the editor, admin API, HTTP nodes and Dashboard.
- Never expose port 1880 to the internet.
- Hunt for unknown flows containing `http in` → `exec` chains, files `/dev/shm/.b`, `/tmp/.kworker` and `/tmp/.s`, and connections to the IPs above plus source `4.201.105[.]116`.

Sources: [VulnCheck](https://vulncheck.com/blog/exploitation-targeting-node-red) · [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/attackers-exploit-critical-siemens.html)

### 1.4 GitLab: 1,000+ servers unpatched for KEV-listed CVE-2026-97229, 11,300+ exposed to CVE-2026-110264 (ONGOING)
**Exposure (Shadowserver, about 39,000 GitLab instances seen daily):**
- **CVE-2026-97229:** more than **1,000** instances are still unpatched. The flaw is a CI/CD regex-engine RCE (Onigmo), it is exploited and in KEV, and the federal deadline lapsed on 1 Nov.
- **CVE-2026-110264:** more than **11,300** (about 30%) are exposed to the related unauthenticated RCE in the same regex compiler. No exploitation is confirmed.

**Fixed versions:** the 21 Oct releases **19.5.1, 19.4.3, 19.3.6, 19.2.9, 19.1.9, 19.0.10 and 18.11.13** fix both flaws. The earlier builds **19.4.2, 19.3.5 and 19.2.8 fix only CVE-2026-97229**.

**Custom images:** Alpine Linux's incident (1 Nov) showed that custom images also need **Ruby 3.3.13**.

Action:
- Upgrade to the 21 Oct releases or later, not just "a 97229 fix", and confirm the bundled Ruby version on custom builds.
- Review CI configurations and logs for unusual regex patterns and worker crashes since 20 Oct.
- Rotate runner tokens, deploy keys and CI secrets on any instance that ran an older build while exposed.

Sources: [Techzine](https://www.techzine.eu/news/security/145456/thousands-of-gitlab-servers-still-unpatched-against-critical-vulnerabilities/) · [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/over-1-000-gitlab-servers-vulnerable-to-code-execution-attacks/) · [CISA KEV](https://www.cisa.gov/known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog)

### 1.5 Synology warns of C3RB3R ransomware hitting outdated DSM (follow-up)
**The warning:** Synology published a rare in-the-wild attack advisory (its first since 2019) on 2 Nov. Attackers are exploiting a vulnerability patched in late 2024 on NAS devices running outdated **DSM**.
- **Payload:** files are renamed `.L0CK3D`, and a `read-me3.txt` note sells a "C3rb3r Decryptor".
- **Which flaw:** Synology points to **Synology-SA-24:20** (**CVE-2024-10441** code execution, **CVE-2024-50629** file read, **CVE-2024-10445** file write) but has not said which one is exploited. Chinese firm 360 instead blames **CVE-2026-13639/13684** (**SA-26:13**). Treat both as in scope.
- **Fixed builds:** DSM **7.2.2-72806-1**, **7.2.1-69057-6**, **7.2-64570-4**, **7.1.1-42962-7** and **6.2.4-25556-8** or later.
- **Exposure:** Shadowserver sees about **380,000** internet-exposed Synology NAS devices.
- **Unknowns:** no indicators of compromise and no decryptor have been published.

Action:
- Update DSM to the latest build for your branch, which covers both advisories, and enable automatic updates.
- Block DSM management from the internet.
- Keep offline or immutable backups that the NAS cannot write to.

Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/synology-warns-customers-of-ongoing-c3rb3r-ransomware-attacks/) · [GridinSoft](https://blog.gridinsoft.com/synology-c3rb3r-ransomware-nas-backups/)
