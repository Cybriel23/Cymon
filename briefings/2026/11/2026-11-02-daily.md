## 1. Risk Watch

### 1.1 Ivanti Connect Secure CVE-2026-111357: federal deadline today (ONGOING)
**CVE-2026-111357** (CVSS **8.8**) is the root-escalation link in the **UTA0576** chain. It follows the authentication bypass **CVE-2026-104935** (CVSS 8.6) and works alongside the predictable-identifier file write **CVE-2026-118527** (CVSS 4.3).
- **Deadlines:** CISA's KEV due date for CVE-2026-111357 is **2 Nov**. CVE-2026-118527 is due **13 Nov**.
- **Naming mismatch:** the KEV entry for CVE-2026-111357 is titled "Missing Authentication for Critical Function", while Ivanti and Volexity describe it as privilege escalation through DSCache trust.
- **Fixed versions:** multiple secondary sources now converge on **25.1.1.3** (affected through 25.1.1.2) and **22.8R2.6** (affected through 22.8R2.5). One source also lists **25.1.3.1**. Ivanti's own advisory feed was not reachable for confirmation, so **verify against Ivanti's advisory** before closing tickets.
- **Attacker behaviour:** no webshell. The actor steals credentials, AD metadata and BitLocker recovery keys, and memory-only changes evaded Ivanti's Integrity Checker.
- **End of life:** 22.7 and 22.8 reach end of life on **31 Jan 2027**.

Action: Patch to 25.1.1.3 or 22.8R2.6 (or later) today and confirm the build on each appliance. Hunt for `X-DSCGI-PATH` requests to `welcome.cgi`, files under `/tmp/` and `/data/.icscap/`, and traffic to `108.165.147[.]64`. Do not treat a clean Integrity Checker result as proof of no compromise. On any exposed appliance, rotate AD service-account, LAPS and BitLocker secrets.

- **Tron:** SomaXBT estimates about **$242M** moved off Tron in two days.

### 1.3 Linux kernel CVE-2026-98432: Ubuntu still unpatched; AWS publishes bulletin (follow-up)
**Ubuntu status:** the tracker, last updated **30 Oct**, still shows no USN.
- **Fixes in progress:** linux **5.15.0-203** (22.04), **6.8.0-149** (24.04), **7.0.0-43** (26.04) and **7.3.0-10** (26.10). The aws and azure kernels are in the same state.
- **No fix in progress:** 20.04, 18.04 and 16.04 are listed as vulnerable with nothing under way.

**Elsewhere:**
- **AWS** issued bulletin **2026-160-AWS** for the flaw on 30 Oct.
- Secondary reports say **Debian**, **Oracle Linux** and **Amazon Linux** have fixes. Red Hat lists RHEL 8, 9 and 10 as affected.

The bug is a remote IPv4 TCP use-after-free (CNA CVSS **9.8**), with an Anthropic-demonstrated remote root against an MPTCP listener. Go 1.24+ services open MPTCP listeners by default. No in-the-wild exploitation is reported.

Action:
- Prioritise internet-facing Go services on Ubuntu and RHEL. Apply vendor kernels as soon as USNs or RHSAs land.
- Until then, restrict exposed TCP services or apply the interim nftables rule that drops IPv4 TCP packets carrying IP options.
- Disabling MPTCP only reduces exposure; it does not remove the bug.

Sources: [Ubuntu CVE tracker](https://ubuntu.com/security/CVE-2026-98432) · [AWS bulletin 2026-160](https://aws.amazon.com/security/security-bulletins/2026-160-aws/) · [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/critical-linux-kernel-flaw-allows-remote-root-attacks-on-mptcp-servers/)

### 1.5 Canonical LXD / MicroCloud CVE-2026-112816 (CVSS 10.0): trusted-client impersonation (NEW)
**The flaw:** LXD validates the whole TLS client certificate chain but only proves possession of the **leaf** key. An attacker can therefore attach a trusted client's public certificate to their own chain and be accepted as that client.
- **Impact:** if the impersonated client is privileged, the attacker gains root on the host.
- **Exposure:** standalone LXD is exposed only when `core.https_address` is set. In **MicroCloud**, a member certificate captured during cluster join is enough.
- **Origin:** the Incus project found the bug. Incus is fixed in **7.6.0**.
- **Fixed versions:** LXD **6.10.1, 5.21.9, 5.0.11 and 4.0.15**; MicroCloud **3.4.1 and 2.1.5**.
- **Exploitation:** no known exploitation or public PoC, and CISA's 30 Oct assessment says it is not exploited.
- **Workaround:** none for LXD.

Action: Run `snap refresh lxd` and `snap refresh microcloud` and confirm the installed version, since some fixes were still moving from candidate to stable. Until patched, restrict the LXD HTTPS API to management networks. Audit the trust store for unexpected client certificates.

Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/critical-lxd-and-microcloud-flaw-cvss.html)
