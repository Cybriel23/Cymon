## 1. Risk Watch

### 1.1 Ivanti Connect Secure CVE-2026-111357: federal deadline tomorrow; fixed versions still inconsistent (ONGOING)
**CVE-2026-111357** (CVSS **8.8**) is the privilege-escalation step in the **UTA0576** chain:
- **The chain:** it combines with the authentication bypass **CVE-2026-104935** (CVSS 8.6) and the predictable-identifier file-write flaw **CVE-2026-118527** (CVSS 4.3). Together they give an unauthenticated attacker root on Connect Secure.
- **Attacker behaviour:** Volexity and THN say the actor deploys no webshell or malware. It steals credentials and AD metadata and hunts for admin passwords and BitLocker recovery keys, so file-based IOC sweeps alone will miss it.
- **Deadlines:** **2 Nov** for CVE-2026-111357 and **13 Nov** for CVE-2026-118527.
- **Version conflict:** THN reports **25.1.1.3** and **22.8R2.6** as fixed. Earlier secondary reporting cited 25.1.1.2, 25.1.3.x and 22.8R2.5. **Verify against Ivanti's own advisory** before marking appliances as remediated.

Action: Move to Ivanti's latest 29 Oct+ build for your release train, then confirm the version against the vendor advisory. Hunt using the indicators from the 31 Oct briefing: `X-DSCGI-PATH` requests to `welcome.cgi`, files under `/tmp/` and `/data/.icscap/`, and traffic to `108.165.147[.]64`. Do not treat a clean Integrity Checker result as proof of no compromise. Where exposure is possible, rotate AD service-account, LAPS and BitLocker secrets.

Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/attackers-chain-three-ivanti-connect.html) · [Security Affairs](https://securityaffairs.com/202175/security/u-s-cisa-adds-ivanti-connect-secure-flaws-to-its-known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog.html) · [CISA KEV catalog](https://www.cisa.gov/known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog)

### 1.2 "Black October": Northflank breach and LLMJacking campaign against AI developer platforms (DEVELOPING)
**What happened at Northflank:**
- **Timeline:** Wiz alerted the cloud hosting platform at **11:43 UTC on 30 Oct**. Northflank told customers to "**assume all environment variables have been decrypted**" and to disable or rotate all keys.
- **Abuse:** on **31 Oct** it confirmed that several customers had seen their stored AI API keys used without authorisation.
- **Unknowns:** the root cause and the number of affected customers have not been disclosed.

**The wider campaign (Wiz):**
- **Nine platforms hit in October:** Fireworks AI, Andromeda, Zeabur (which is shutting down), Lightning AI, OpenCode, Railway, Warp, CommandCode and Northflank. Several reported "agent swarm" style intrusions that hunted stored environment variables.
- **What happens to stolen keys:** they are validated within about 30 minutes, checked repeatedly for days, then resold through LLM broker services. Wiz links key usage to **"momo" (llm-routing[.]com)** and **"Xingjiabi" (xingjiabiapi[.]org)**.
- **Scale:** in two cases, keys were used for bulk Amazon Bedrock abuse of **~800k** and **~1.7M** calls, more than **$100k** at list price.
- **Attribution:** no single vulnerability or actor has been confirmed.

Action:
- If you used any of the nine platforms, rotate every secret stored there, not only AI keys.
- Review AI-provider and cloud billing and usage logs from **18 Oct** onward.
- Block and hunt for the key-checking IPs `23.106.46[.]239`, `38.246.114[.]118`, `104.243.32[.]50` and `31.57.201[.]201`.
- Prefer short-lived, scoped credentials over long-lived provider keys in PaaS environment variables.

Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/northflank-security-breach-leads-to-abuse-of-customers-ai-api-keys/) · [Wiz](https://www.wiz.io/blog/black-october-for-ai-developer-platforms)

### 1.3 Binance theft: FBI attributes it to TraderTraitor and publishes laundering addresses; withdrawals restarting (follow-up)
**FBI attribution (31 Oct):**
- **Who:** the FBI formally attributed the theft of "more than **$6 billion**" from Binance (around 29 Oct) to the DPRK cluster it tracks as **TraderTraitor**, the same label it gave the 2025 Bybit theft.
- **Laundering:** the funds are being converted mainly into ETH.
- **Ask to industry:** together with USSS and IRS-CI, the FBI asked node operators, bridges, DeFi services and exchanges to block about **600+ ETH, ~20 BTC and 1 SOL** laundering addresses.

**Binance's position:**
- **Losses covered:** **90%** of the stolen assets are now backed, from SAFU, its Futures insurance fund and its corporate treasury, and it will absorb the loss.
- **Withdrawals:** the first phase restarts today, between **15:00 and 18:00 KST**, subject to security checks.
- **How it happened:** co-CEO Richard Teng now describes a "complex attack" involving a compromised laptop and malware built to delete evidence.
- **Frozen funds:** stablecoin issuers have frozen more than **$2.4B**.

Action:
- Crypto exchanges, OTC desks and DeFi front-ends should ingest the FBI address list into screening now.
- Every organisation should treat this as a reminder of TraderTraitor's playbook: social engineering of DevOps and cloud engineers, then abuse of build and signing infrastructure. Review privileged-engineer endpoint controls and hot-wallet signing paths.
- Expect phishing that impersonates Binance "refund" or "recovery" processes.

Sources: [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/policy/2026/10/31/fbi-confirms-north-korea-s-role-in-usd6-billion-binance-hack-asks-defi-community-to-block-btc-eth-sol-addresses) · [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/1/fbi-blames-north-korea-for-record-6-3bn-crypto-heist) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/31/binance-hack-asset-coverage-withdrawals.html) · [Bloomingbit (Binance notice)](https://en.bloomingbit.io/feed/news/123376)

### 1.4 Linux kernel CVE-2026-98432: Ubuntu still has no released fix (follow-up)
Ubuntu's tracker, last updated **30 Oct**, still lists every supported release as vulnerable with no USN yet:
- **22.04:** fix in progress in linux 5.15.0-203.
- **24.04:** fix in progress in linux 6.8.0-149.
- **26.04:** fix in progress in linux 7.0.0-43.
- **26.10:** fix in progress in linux 7.3.0-10.

Several variants show "Vulnerable" with no fix in progress, among them some nvidia, raspi, realtime and FIPS kernels. Red Hat and Rocky fixes are still planned. This is the remote IPv4 TCP use-after-free (CNA CVSS **9.8**). Anthropic showed a remote root exploit against an MPTCP listener, and Go 1.24+ services open MPTCP listeners by default. No exploitation has been reported in the wild.

Action:
- Inventory internet-facing Ubuntu and RHEL hosts, especially Go services.
- Until kernels ship, restrict exposed TCP services or apply CIQ's interim nftables rule that drops IPv4 TCP packets carrying IP options.
- Watch for USNs early this week.

Sources: [Ubuntu CVE tracker](https://ubuntu.com/security/CVE-2026-98432) · [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/critical-linux-kernel-flaw-allows-remote-root-attacks-on-mptcp-servers/)

### 1.5 Android "SMS of Death" CVE-2026-58882: public PoC crashes the phone process with one message (NEW)
**The bug:** researcher Mark Blah published an analysis and PoC for a zero-click denial-of-service in Android's `WapPushOverSms.decodeWapPdu`.
- **Trigger:** an 8-byte malformed WAP Push data SMS to port **2948** produces a negative header length and crashes `com.android.phone`.
- **Impact:** the crash cuts cellular service and drops calls, **including emergency calls**. About 20 messages can cause a reboot loop; one Android 12 device needed a factory reset.
- **Fix status:** fixed for **Android 14–17** in the October 2026 updates, although the fix is not listed in Google's October bulletin. **Android 12 and 13 did not receive the fix.**

Action:
- Push October 2026 patches across your mobile fleet.
- Identify Android 12 and 13 devices, especially those used by on-call or field staff, and plan replacement or alternative communications.
- Ask your carrier whether it filters malformed WAP Push messages.

Sources: [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/new-android-sms-of-death-exploit-can-remotely-crash-unpatched-phones/)

## 4. Threat Watch
- **TraderTraitor (DPRK) formally named for Binance:**
  - **Pattern:** TRM Labs points to the same patterns seen in Bybit and KelpDAO: an operator wallet funded through small Tornado Cash withdrawals, and transaction signatures collected in advance and executed in one burst.
  - **Context:** with the FBI's attribution, North Korea's 2026 crypto theft total is now its largest on record.
  - **Watch for:** laundering through THORChain, cross-chain bridges and OTC desks in the coming days.

  [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/policy/2026/10/31/fbi-confirms-north-korea-s-role-in-usd6-billion-binance-hack-asks-defi-community-to-block-btc-eth-sol-addresses) · [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/31/binance-hack-asset-coverage-withdrawals.html)
- **VOLTA-AUTODOXER infostealer (Talos, 31 Oct):**
  - **AI triage:** a builder-model Windows stealer that sends a compiled "Identity_Report" to **OpenRouter** for an AI-written "target assessment". The assessment ranks the victim's monetisable assets and security posture.
  - **Models:** defaults to Claude Opus 4.6, falling back to Kimi K2 and then Gemini 2.5 Flash when a model refuses.
  - **What it steals:** browser credentials and cards, Discord, Telegram and Steam sessions, Microsoft OAuth tokens, SSH keys and cloud configs, Wi-Fi passwords and crypto wallets (with a built-in vault cracker). It also uses stolen Gmail sessions to confirm home addresses ("doxxing").
  - **IOCs:** SHA-256 `57ef4242666d78c50df145b34023df564f0fad38812d6a580e66433d427e2279`, plus the artefacts `volta_debug.txt`, `Identity_Report.txt` and `AI_Analysis.txt`.
  - **Detection:** alert on headless Chrome launched by unexpected processes, and on unexpected endpoint traffic to `openrouter.ai/api/v1/chat/completions`.

  [Cisco Talos](https://blog.talosintelligence.com/appraisal-by-ai-model-driven-victim-prioritization-in-the-volta-infostealer/)
