## 1. Risk Watch

### 1.1 Ivanti Connect Secure chain, CVE-2026-104935 (ONGOING, follow-up)
Volexity attributes the chain to **UTA0576**, which gained unauthenticated root on a fully patched appliance running **22.8R2.4**. The auth bypass and RCE (CVE-2026-104935, CVSS **8.6**) are fixed in builds reported as **22.8R2.5, 25.1.1.2, 25.1.3.0 and 25.1.3.1**. The third link, a privilege escalation in the dsinvoked broker's handling of DSCache configuration data, reportedly has **no CVE and no fix**. The actor reportedly deployed no malware or web shell, tampered with logs, and ran in memory, so Ivanti's Integrity Checker Tool may miss it. Start dates differ by source (Volexity says 3 Oct; others say 1 Oct or earlier). I found no new Ivanti release in the last 24 hours. One source advises moving to the 25.x branch because 22.x is nearing end of life.
**Action:** Upgrade to a fixed build and watch Ivanti's advisory for the escalation fix; remove internet exposure of management and portal interfaces where possible; treat pre-patch exposure as potential compromise, capture memory and network forensics, and rotate credentials and certificates held on the device.
Sources: [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/attacker-chains-three-ivanti-connect.html) · [BleepingComputer](https://bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/ivanti-warns-of-connect-secure-zero-day-exploited-in-attacks) · [SOCRadar](https://socradar.io/blog/cve-2026-104935-ivanti-connect-secure/) · [Cybernews](https://cybernews.com/security/ivanti-zero-day-july-cisa-patch-no-fix/)

### 1.2 CISA KEV, 16 Oct: BeyondTrust Remote Support CVE-2026-102764 and Fortinet CVE-2025-61624 (NEW, partly unverified)
A search summary of CISA's 16 Oct alert says two entries were added, both with evidence of exploitation: **CVE-2026-102764**, a BeyondTrust Remote Support authentication bypass by spoofing, and **CVE-2025-61624**, a Fortinet path traversal affecting multiple products. I could not load the alert or find any record of CVE-2026-102764; the nearest BeyondTrust IDs are **CVE-2026-40138** (RS and PRA, CVSS v3 **8.1**) and **CVE-2026-40139** (CVSS v4 **9.2**, fixed in **25.3.3**), and I found no exploitation confirmation for those. CVE-2025-61624 is described by secondary sources as a FortiOS CLI path traversal (also FortiPAM, FortiProxy, FortiSwitchManager; Fortinet advisory FG-IR-26-122, CVSS **5.4** to **6.5**) that needs admin credentials, with a virtual patch (FG-VD-59270.0day, FMWP update **25.120**) offered. Treat the IDs and status as unconfirmed until checked against the KEV catalog.
**Action:** Check the live KEV catalog for both entries and their due dates; patch self-hosted BeyondTrust Remote Support to **25.3.3** or later; apply Fortinet fixes and restrict administrative access to Fortinet management interfaces; review admin sessions and file changes.
Sources: [Wiz on CVE-2025-61624](https://www.wiz.io/vulnerability-database/cve/cve-2025-61624) · [Stack.watch](https://stack.watch/vuln/CVE-2025-61624/) · [NVD CVE-2026-40139](https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/CVE-2026-40139) · [Cyber Security News on BeyondTrust](https://cybersecuritynews.com/beyondtrust-vulnerabilities/amp/)

### 1.3 European supermarket refrigeration attacks (DEVELOPING, follow-up)
Remote attacks on **Danfoss** refrigeration controllers forced defrost cycles and pushed temperatures to about **50 °C**. French Finance Minister Roland Lescure put the toll at about **700 of 10,000** French supermarkets; Mousquetaires reports **300** Intermarché/Netto stores, Coopérative U fewer than **100**. Danfoss says most affected units are legacy products that no longer get security updates. Reuters counted at least **4,500** Danfoss interfaces visible on Shodan. The Paris prosecutor's cybercrime unit leads the investigation, ANSSI is involved and authorities are checking other countries. The intrusion method is not established, and I found no claim of responsibility, ransom demand or arrest. CISA previously warned of Danfoss AK-SM 8xxA flaws (**CVE-2025-41450** auth bypass, **CVE-2025-41451** command injection), but no source ties them to these attacks.
**Action:** Find internet-reachable refrigeration and building-management controllers, take them off direct exposure (VPN or allow-list), isolate end-of-life units, change default or shared credentials and check logs for unexpected defrost or setpoint changes.
Sources: [Cybernews](https://cybernews.com/security/danfoss-refrigeration-hack-france-supermarkets/) · [BleepingComputer](https://bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/cyberattack-shuts-down-refrigerators-at-french-supermarkets) · [Security Affairs](https://securityaffairs.com/?p=200863)

### 1.4 SonicWall SMA 1000 and IDCF Cloud (ONGOING, follow-ups)
**SonicWall:** The pre-auth SSRF rated CVSS **10.0** is cited as **CVE-2026-102255** in earlier coverage, but other sources use **CVE-2026-15409** (with **CVE-2026-15410**, a post-auth code injection, CVSS **7.2**) and cite fixed builds **12.4.3-03453 / 12.5.0-02835**; yesterday's briefing gave different build numbers, so confirm IDs and builds in SonicWall's advisory. Exploitation attempts were seen from **8 Oct**; successful breaches are not established. **IDCF Cloud (SoftBank), Japan:** the 7 Oct attack hit four East Japan Region 1 zones, **495** organisations, and management consoles remain disabled in all regions while security is verified; staff restore virtual servers manually on request. JR East reports email addresses of up to **6.09 million** records may have leaked (single non-English source). The attackers' claim of a seven-minute intrusion and deletion of **550,000+** snapshots is unconfirmed. Entry route and actor are undisclosed.
**Action:** SonicWall: upgrade and limit WorkPlace exposure. IDCF customers: rebuild from offline backups; keep backups and hypervisor credentials outside the provider's management plane.
Sources: [Tenable](https://tenable.com/blog/cve-2026-15409-cve-2026-15410-sonicwall-sma-1000-zero-day-vulnerabilities-exploited-in-the) · [Security Affairs](https://securityaffairs.com/195364/hacking/sonicwall-warns-of-active-exploitation-of-two-sma-1000-zero-days.html) · [SDxCentral](https://www.sdxcentral.com/news/softbank-national-cloud-arm-attacked-in-seven-minutes-taking-japan-infrastructure-offline/) · [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/ransomware-attack-disrupts-japans-idcf-cloud-used-by-govt-clients/)
